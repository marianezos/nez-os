# NEZ OS · The Agent Operating System

AI and GoHighLevel systems for real estate agents, built around a Notion brain that knows your business. By **Maria Nez AI**.

NEZ OS gives your AI (Claude or ChatGPT) a brain: one place in Notion that holds who you are, your market, your clients, your voice, your rules and your goals. NEZ OS skills read that brain, so everything they write sounds like you and follows your rules.

## What you need

1. A **Notion** account (free works).
2. The **NEZ OS Brain** template, duplicated into your Notion (link in the NEZ OS Vault).
3. **Claude** with the Notion connector turned on and access to your NEZ OS Brain page.

## Install

1. In Claude, open the plugins area and choose to add a plugin marketplace.
2. Paste this repository's link: `https://github.com/marianezos/nez-os`
3. Install **NEZ OS**.
4. Start a new chat and run `/nez-setup`.

## The five pillars

**Brain** (who you are) · **Leads** (how people find you) · **Clients** (who you're helping) · **Content** (what you share) · **Growth** (where you're going)
