# Starter objections: Commercial (sales and leases)

NEZ OS starters for Stage 5. Rewrite each answer in the agent's voice, swap [PROOF] for one of their proof points (or [ADD PROOF]), and add the pushback they hear most.

**Note:** commercial deals often have different license, disclosure and contract rules. Only use these if the agent does commercial work. Lease terms, zoning, taxes and legal questions go to their attorney, CPA or the city. Still never describe or target people by a protected class.

## 1. "The rent or price is too high."
- **Category:** Price
- **Behind it:** They're comparing the headline number, not the total cost.
- **Ask first:** "What total monthly cost works for your business, including everything?"
- **Answer idea:** Compare options on the same basis: base rent or price, extra charges, build-out, length of term and incentives. Then negotiate where there's room. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Let's compare total cost, not just the headline number. Want me to line up your options side by side?
- **Don't say:** Legal interpretations of lease terms.

## 2. "I'll deal with the owner directly."
- **Category:** Other agent
- **Behind it:** They want to save money or keep things simple.
- **Ask first:** "Who's looking out for your side of the terms if you go direct?"
- **Answer idea:** Explain what you do for them: finding options, comparing terms, negotiating concessions and keeping the timeline on track. [PROOF]
- **Text:** You can go direct. If you'd like someone on your side of the terms, I'm happy to help.
- **Don't say:** Anything negative about the owner or their broker.

## 3. "I'm not sure how long a commitment to make."
- **Category:** Timing
- **Behind it:** Uncertainty about growth.
- **Ask first:** "Where do you see the business in two to three years?"
- **Answer idea:** Explain the trade-offs between short and long terms, and options they can ask their attorney about, like renewal or expansion terms. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Let's map out where the business is headed, then find terms that fit. Free this week?
- **Don't say:** Legal advice.

## 4. "Let's wait until business picks up."
- **Category:** Timing
- **Behind it:** Cash flow worries.
- **Ask first:** "What would need to happen for you to feel ready?"
- **Answer idea:** Respect it. Offer to keep watching options that fit their criteria and to check back on a date they choose. [PROOF]
- **Text:** No rush. Want me to keep an eye out and check back on a date that works for you?
- **Don't say:** Predictions about the market or their business.
