# Starter objections: Renters

NEZ OS starters for Stage 5. Rewrite each answer in the agent's voice, swap [PROOF] for one of their proof points (or [ADD PROOF]), and add the pushback they hear most. Be honest about all costs. Never promise approval for an application.

## 1. "I'll just search on my own."
- **Category:** Other agent
- **Behind it:** They don't see the value, or worry about a fee.
- **Ask first:** "What's been the hardest part of your search so far?"
- **Answer idea:** Searching alone works for many people. Explain clearly what you do and what it costs them, if anything, so there are no surprises. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Totally fine to search on your own. If it helps, I can send places that fit your budget and explain any fees up front.
- **Don't say:** Hidden fees. "Landlords won't talk to you without an agent."

## 2. "The move-in costs are too much."
- **Category:** Money & readiness
- **Behind it:** Deposit, first month and fees add up fast.
- **Ask first:** "What total amount can you comfortably have ready to move in?"
- **Answer idea:** Lay out every move-in cost up front so they can plan. Then search for places that fit that total, not just the monthly rent. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Let's plan around the total move-in cost, not just rent. What amount can you have ready?
- **Don't say:** Ways to hide income or stretch the truth on an application.

## 3. "I'm worried about my credit."
- **Category:** Money & readiness
- **Behind it:** Fear of being turned down.
- **Ask first:** "Do you know what the places you like ask for?"
- **Answer idea:** Each landlord sets its own written criteria. Offer to find out the requirements before they apply, so they don't waste application fees. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Before you pay any application fees, I can find out what each place requires. Want me to check?
- **Don't say:** "You'll get approved." Credit advice.

## 4. "Why not just buy?"
- **Category:** Money & readiness
- **Behind it:** They're wondering whether renting is the right move.
- **Ask first:** "Is buying something you'd like in the next year or two?"
- **Answer idea:** Renting can be the right step for now. If buying is a goal, offer to connect them with a lender to see what it would take, with no pressure. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Renting may be right for now. If buying's on your list, want a no-pressure chat with a lender to see what it would take?
- **Don't say:** "Renting is throwing money away."
