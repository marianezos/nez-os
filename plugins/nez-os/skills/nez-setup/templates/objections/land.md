# Starter objections: Land

NEZ OS starters for Stage 5. Rewrite each answer in the agent's voice, swap [PROOF] for one of their proof points (or [ADD PROOF]), and add the pushback they hear most.

**Note:** zoning, permits, utilities, soil and surveys are for the city, county and licensed pros to confirm. Never promise what can be built.

## 1. "I'll just buy it and figure out what to build later."
- **Category:** Trust & process
- **Behind it:** They don't know what could get in the way.
- **Ask first:** "What do you hope to build or do with it?"
- **Answer idea:** Explain what to check first: zoning, utilities, access, survey and soil or septic needs, with the city or county and licensed pros. Offer to build those checks into the offer timeline. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Before you buy, let's make sure you can do what you want with it. Want a quick list of what to check?
- **Don't say:** "You can build anything there."

## 2. "The price is too high for raw land."
- **Category:** Price
- **Behind it:** Few sales to compare, so the price feels random.
- **Ask first:** "What would make the price feel fair to you?"
- **Answer idea:** Show the closest land sales and what's different: size, access, utilities, zoning. Decide on an offer together. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Let's look at nearby land sales and what makes each one different. Want me to pull them?
- **Don't say:** Value promises.

## 3. "I want to sell my land myself."
- **Category:** Trust & process
- **Behind it:** They think land is easy to sell.
- **Ask first:** "What kind of buyer do you think wants it?"
- **Answer idea:** Explain how you find the right buyer for that kind of land and help them answer the questions buyers will ask about zoning, access and utilities. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Happy to help either way. Want to know what buyers usually ask about land like yours?
- **Don't say:** Guarantees about who will buy or for how much.

## 4. "There's no rush; land doesn't go anywhere."
- **Category:** Timing
- **Behind it:** No pressure to decide.
- **Ask first:** "Is there a date you'd like it sold or bought by?"
- **Answer idea:** Agree there's no rush. Offer to keep watching and check in on their schedule. [PROOF]
- **Text:** No rush. I'll keep an eye out and check in when you'd like.
- **Don't say:** False urgency.
