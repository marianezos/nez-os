# NEZ OS Setup interview

Ask one question at a time. The wording below is a guide: say it naturally and adapt to what the agent already told you. Skip any question they've already answered. Lines in *italics* are notes for you, not things to say.

---

## Stage 1: You and your market

1. What name do you use in your business? (The one on your signs and social media.)
2. Which brokerage are you with?
3. How long have you been licensed, and in which state or states?
4. What languages do you work in?
5. What did you do before real estate? *(Experience from another career is often their best story. Save it.)*
6. Why did you get into real estate? Give me the real reason, not the bio version.
7. Which cities or neighborhoods do you serve most?
8. What's your typical price range?
9. If a friend moving to your area asked what makes it special, what would you tell them? *(Places, lifestyle, commute, things to do. If they describe people, reword to describe the place and explain why in one sentence.)*

**Saves to Master Profile:** About me (name, brokerage, license/state, years, languages, why, before), My market (cities, price range, what makes it special).

---

## Stage 2: Your clients and what makes you different

1. Who do you love working with most? (First-time buyers, move-up buyers, sellers, investors, relocation, luxury, and so on.)
2. What are they usually most worried about when they first talk to you?
3. What do they need to hear from you to feel confident?
4. What do you do better or differently than most agents?
5. Do you have a niche or specialty? It's fine if not yet.
6. Tell me your story in a few sentences: where you started, a turning point, and why you do this now.
7. Walk me through what working with you looks like for a **buyer**, from first call to keys.
8. And for a **seller**: what's included in your marketing, and what are the main steps?
9. Any programs you know really well? (First-time buyer programs, down payment assistance, VA, FHA, and so on.)
10. Do you offer any free guides, tools, classes or events?

**Saves to:** Master Profile (My ideal clients, What makes me different), Offers & Services (For buyers, For sellers, Other clients, Free resources, Classes or events).

*Never write promises or guarantees into Offers & Services. If they say "I'll sell your home in 30 days," save it as their goal or process, not a guarantee, and mention why.*

---

## Stage 3: Your brand and your rules

1. What are your brand name(s) and tagline, if you have them?
2. Your brand colors and fonts? (Hex codes are great if you have them. "Navy and gold" is fine too.)
3. Your website and social media handles?
4. Words or phrases you always use?
5. Words you never want to see in anything written for you?
6. How would you describe your tone in a few words? (For example: warm and direct, fun and casual, polished and calm.)
7. How does your brokerage name have to appear on marketing, exactly?
8. Does your state or brokerage require any license number, disclosure text or the Equal Housing Opportunity logo on ads?
9. Any other rules from your state, MLS or brokerage I should always follow? (For example: no "coming soon" ads, team naming rules, texting rules.)

**Saves to:** Master Profile (My brand, Required on all marketing), Compliance Guardrails (My state and brokerage rules).

*Tell them: "These rules get checked on every draft. You're still responsible for final compliance, so double-check anything new with your broker."*

---

## Stage 4: Your goals

1. What's your gross commission income goal for this year?
2. What's your average sale price, roughly?
3. What's your average commission per side (percentage or dollars)?
4. What's your split and any fees (brokerage split, cap, transaction fees)?
5. Where does most of your business come from today? (Sphere, referrals, open houses, online leads, social media, and so on.)
6. Do you know your conversion rates, like how many conversations it takes to get an appointment? *(If no, use the estimates in SKILL.md and label them.)*
7. What do you want **more** of this year?
8. What do you want **less** of?
9. What are your top 3 priorities for this quarter?

**Do the math in chat, step by step:**
- Net per closing = average price × commission per side × (1 − split) − per-deal fees
- Closings needed = income goal ÷ net per closing (round up)
- Then work backward to contracts, appointments, conversations and leads per year, month and week.

**Saves to:** Master Profile (My goals this year), Business Plan & Goals (The big picture, The math table, Top lead sources, This quarter).

---

## Stage 5: Your tools

1. What CRM do you use? (GoHighLevel, Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, a spreadsheet, none yet?)
2. Which AI tools do you use? (Claude, ChatGPT, others.)
3. Which social platforms are you active on?
4. What do you use for email, website, video or design (for example Canva, CapCut)?
5. Anything for transactions or e-signatures?
6. Which hours do you work, and which hours do you protect for family or yourself?
7. What's the best way for clients to reach you: text, call or email?

**Saves to:** Software Stack (one row per tool: Tool, Category, Used For; check "Connected to AI" only if they say it's connected to their AI), Master Profile (How I work).

*Never ask for or save passwords or logins.*

---

## Stage 6: Your areas

1. Let's make a fact file for each area you serve. Which one should we start with?
2. Is it a city, neighborhood, subdivision or zip code?
3. What do you love about it, or what do buyers usually ask about? (Parks, commute, shopping, things to do, housing styles.) *(Places, not people.)*
4. Do you have current numbers for it, like median price or days on market, and where they're from? *(Only save numbers they give you with a source and date.)*
5. Want to add another area?

Repeat for each area. Two or three is plenty for a first pass.

**Saves to:** Market Areas (one row per area: Area, Type, Highlights; numbers only with source and date).

*Tell them they can add more areas any time by saying "add a market area."*
