# Starting widgets for any business

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 5 when `industry_flow` isn't `real-estate`. Suggestions only, offered after discovery, never forced. Keep only what they'd act on. Every widget reads from their own apps, or from a brain database they approved.

## How the menu uses this

The menu offers separate dashboards: the Command Center (Today, Email, Tasks, and a card for each other dashboard), CRM (if they have one), Content, Money, and one industry dashboard from below.

## For almost everyone

- **Today:** appointments and calls from the calendar.
- **Email:** emails that need a reply, with drafted replies in their voice.
- **Tasks:** from their one task home.
- **Important dates:** from the CRM or calendar, or a brain tracker.
- **Pipeline value and clients by stage:** count and value per stage, each client's last touch. Profit only with their own margin or rate, with the math shown and labeled "Potential, not guaranteed." No rate: value only. Never a typical rate.
- **Money snapshot:** the quarter's targets against actual from /nez-goals, and cash in if a finance tool is connected.
- **Marketing snapshot:** the Marketing Hub and Content Planner at a glance.

## Follow-Up Queue (any business with a CRM or a list of people to follow up)

Groups they name (leads, open quotes, past customers), with their stale days and channel per group. Ranked by behavior only, with a one-line reason and a draft in their voice. Text only with consent, otherwise email, otherwise "Call or skip." Full detail in `../references/follow-up-queue.md`.

## A lender or loan officer

- **Pipeline by stage:** applications, in processing, conditions out, clear to close, from their CRM or loan system if connected, or a brain tracker.
- **Conditions and documents:** what's still needed per file, from a brain tracker (Borrower first name, Condition, Requested date, Received, Status). Never show account numbers or financial details beyond the condition name.
- **Rate and closing dates:** from their system or the tracker, never computed.

## A service business (contractor, cleaner, consultant, repair)

- **Leads and quotes:** new leads, quotes waiting, quotes to chase, from the CRM or a brain tracker (Quote, Client, Amount, Sent Date, Status).
- **Jobs this week:** from the calendar, with what's needed for each.
- **Invoices:** unpaid and overdue from the finance tool, with a reminder draft (sent only on click).

## An agency or freelancer with clients

- **Clients:** deliverables due, approvals waiting, last message, from a brain tracker (Client, Deliverable, Due Date, Status, Owner).
- **Client email:** the email widget filtered by client.
- **Voices:** which voice to use for each client's drafts (Voice Library).
- **Invoices and retainers:** from the finance tool.

## A coach, teacher or consultant

- **Sessions today:** from the calendar.
- **Clients needing a follow-up:** from the CRM or a brain tracker (Client, Program, Last Session, Next Step).
- **Program progress:** a brain tracker with steps and status.
- **Payments:** from the finance tool.

## A local practice (dental, clinic, salon, studio)

- **Today's appointments:** from the calendar or practice tool if connected.
- **Recalls and follow-ups due:** a brain tracker unless the CRM holds them.
- **Reviews to answer:** a brain tracker (Platform, Reviewer First Name, Date, Status) with drafts in their voice. Reviews are answered on the platform, never auto-posted.
- **Never show health details.** Names or initials and the next step only.

## An online store

- **Orders and issues:** from the commerce or finance connector.
- **Customer email:** the email widget, filtered to support.
- **Inventory flags:** a brain tracker unless a tool holds it.

## Rules

- Never invent a number or a status. An empty widget says what it needs.
- No sensitive data (health, financial account numbers, private notes) unless they asked for that widget, and then only what it needs.
