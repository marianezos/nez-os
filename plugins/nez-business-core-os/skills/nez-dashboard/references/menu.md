# The menu: what can you have?

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 2, after the scan and before discovery. Agents don't know what a dashboard can show, so suggest first, then run discovery only on what they pick. One short message, one question.

## Build the menu from the scan

Offer only what the scan says is possible. Never offer a dashboard that needs an app they don't have and couldn't plan around.

- **A) Command Center.** Always the first suggestion: the daily feed (today, email, tasks, brain to-dos, and one summary card for each other dashboard they pick).
- **B) CRM.** If GoHighLevel or another CRM is connected or they use one: new and active leads, who needs a follow-up, clients by stage, and potential earnings.
- **C) Content.** If the Content Planner has rows or they plan content: posting today, what needs their review, what's waiting on them, and what's stuck.
- **D) Money.** If My business numbers or Quarterly Goals exist, or a finance tool is connected: the quarter's targets against actual, GCI and take-home.
- **E) Transactions.** For real estate: every file under contract, its key dates, documents needed and what's missing. Other industries: the one that fits from `../industries/generic.md` (for example jobs and quotes, clients and deliverables, recalls and reviews).
- **F) Something else.** A custom tracker they describe.

Letters follow the order shown, so skip a letter rather than leave a gap. Say in the same message which connected apps each one uses, in a few words ("CRM uses GoHighLevel").

## How to ask

> "Here's what I can build for you, based on what I see:
> A) Command Center (today, email, tasks, and a card for each of the others)
> B) CRM (GoHighLevel)
> C) Content
> D) Money
> E) Transactions
> Which do you want? You can pick several. I recommend starting with A. Each one is its own page, so you can add the others later."

Accept "all," a list of letters, or "just A." Never push more than they pick. If they pick only one and it isn't A, say once that the Command Center is where the daily feed lives, and offer it.

## After they pick

- Run discovery (`discovery.md`) only for the picked dashboards.
- Every item still passes "If you saw this, what would you do?"
- Record the picks and the ones they passed on, on the Dashboards rows, so a later update can offer the passed ones once.
