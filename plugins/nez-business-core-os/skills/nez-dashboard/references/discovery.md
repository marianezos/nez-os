# Discovery interview

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 4, after the menu, only for the picked dashboards. Discovery comes first and widgets serve the answers. Scan first and skip anything the Master Profile, My personality, the Dashboards rows and the connected apps already answer. A few questions at a time, and show what you inferred as statements they can correct.

## The questions

1. **First look.** What do you check first every morning? What do you open many times a day?
2. **Slipped through the cracks.** What has slipped lately (a reply, a deadline, a document, a follow-up, an inspection date)? What was it, and how did you find out?
3. **Weekly decisions.** What do you decide each week? What numbers would help you decide faster?
4. **Who and where.** Just you, a team, or an assistant? Do they need the same things? Mostly on your phone or a computer?
5. **What you track now.** Where do you track things today (a spreadsheet, a notebook, GoHighLevel, a board, your head)? Any dashboard you already use? Offer to review one they paste or describe.
6. **One click.** What would you want to do with one click (reply, make a task, mark something done, move something)?
7. **Where tasks live.** "Where do you keep your tasks? A) GoHighLevel B) Asana, ClickUp, Todoist or another app C) Your Tasks database in your brain." Then scan that tool (`tasks.md`). No connector for it: use the Tasks database and say so once. Never split tasks across two places.
8. **Email to task** (when email is connected and the Command Center is picked). Explain: "Every email that needs a reply becomes a task, linked to the thread, and the task closes when you reply or click No reply needed." Ask how it should run: a scheduled task they approve, or on open if scheduled tasks aren't available (`email-tasks.md`).

## Follow-Up Queue questions (when they want follow-ups)

Full detail in `follow-up-queue.md`. Scan the CRM first so the questions use their real pipelines and stages.

1. **Stale:** no contact in how many days, for each group (new leads, active buyers and sellers, past clients, sphere, or their own groups)? Offer starting points (for example 3, 7, 90 and 180 days) and say they're only a starting point.
2. **Pipelines and stages:** which to include, and which to skip (lost, closed, do-not-contact).
3. **Channel:** text or email for each group? (Every text is still checked for consent and A2P per person.)
4. **List size:** how many people at once?
5. **Logging:** "When you send from the dashboard, I log the touch as a note in GoHighLevel." OK?
6. **Freshness:** offer a daily scheduled task that builds the queue and drafts ahead, and ask the time of day.

## The filter (every item)

For every item they mention, ask: "If you saw this, what would you do?"
- A clear action (reply, call, send, approve, chase, decide, delegate): keep it and note the action. Make it a button where the tools allow.
- "Nothing" or "nice to know": drop it, or move it to a small "later" list. Say you're dropping it and why, in one line.
- Depends on a number: write down the number and the threshold ("tell me when it's above X").

## What to capture (for the plan and the Dashboards row)

The first-look list in order; each slip and how it should surface (a widget, an alert, a date reminder); the weekly decisions and their numbers; each kept item with its action and data source; each dropped item with the reason; who uses it and on what device; existing dashboards reviewed; the task home and how email to task runs.

## Rules

- Never suggest a widget they didn't need. Presets are suggestions, offered after discovery.
- Never ask about an app or data they said they don't use.
- Never ask for sensitive data. If they offer some, use only what the widget needs.
- Keep it short. Stop when you can name every widget, its data source and its action.
