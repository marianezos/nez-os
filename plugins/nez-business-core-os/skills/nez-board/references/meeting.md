# How a NEZ OS board meeting runs

## 1. Get the question

Ask the agent what decision or problem they want to bring. If it's vague, ask up to 2 short follow-ups to get:
- The decision in one sentence ("Should I spend $500/month on Facebook ads for seller leads?")
- Any deadline, budget or constraint.

Don't ask for things the brain already has (their market, goals, tools).

## 2. Pull context from the brain

First, check Board Meetings for rows on a similar topic. If one exists, mention its call and outcome ("Last time you asked about ads, the board said Go with changes, and it Worked") so the advisors build on it.

Read, quietly:
- Master Profile (goals, market, clients) and Business Plan & Goals (income goal, the math, lead sources).
- Decisions Log rows that are Still Active and related to the question.
- Active AI Memory rows in the related Area.
- Any related Clients & Deals, Bottlenecks or Marketing Hub rows.

If a past decision already covers this, say so up front and ask whether they want to revisit it.

## 3. Choose the board

- Default: up to 7 Active advisors.
- If more than 7 are Active, pick the 6 whose questions fit this topic best, plus The Skeptic if they're Active, and say who's sitting out.
- The Skeptic speaks last, after hearing the others.
- The agent can name specific advisors ("just ask the Profit Keeper and the Listings Strategist").

## 4. Each advisor speaks

For each advisor, read **only** that advisor's brain page, then write their take:
- **Header:** advisor name and their question.
- **Their take:** 3 to 6 sentences in their voice, using the agent's real numbers and situation.
- **Their test:** the one check from "How they think" that matters most here, and how this idea scores.
- **Verdict:** Yes, No, or Yes if... (with the condition).

Rules:
- Stay in character: use their lens and voice, not a generic answer.
- A Mentor or Public figure speaks from their brain page: their beliefs, tools, positions and stories. When the brain doesn't cover the topic, they apply their principles and lens to it, and say it's how they'd likely see it. Only use exact quotes from their "In their words" section.
- Advisors may disagree. Don't force agreement.
- Keep each advisor under about 120 words so a full board stays readable.
- **Local Market Expert:** before it speaks, also read the Market Areas rows the decision touches. Use only numbers that have a source and date there or that the agent gave; if a needed number is missing or older than 90 days, say so and ask for it rather than estimating.
- **Conversion Coach:** when the decision touches client conversations, also read the Objection Bank rows it touches. Lean on answers marked Works, flag Starter or Testing answers as untested, and suggest adding any new objection that comes up as a Testing row.

## 5. Summary

After all advisors:
- **Where they agree:** one or two lines.
- **Where they disagree:** one line per disagreement, naming who's on each side.
- **Blind-spot check:** if the majority view leans on an advisor's known blind spot (from "Where they can be wrong"), say so.
- **The call:** Go, No go, Go with changes, or Need more info.
- **Recommendation:** one clear recommendation and the first step to take this week. Say it's a recommendation and the decision is theirs.
- **What would change the answer:** one or two facts that would flip it.

## 6. Save

Ask: "Want me to save this meeting?" If yes:
- **Board Meetings:** one row. Decision: the question in a few words. Date: now (date and time). Call: the board's call. Advisors: the names of those who sat. Pillar: the closest pillar. Follow-up: 30 days out by default (sooner for quick tests, longer for big moves; ask if unsure). Outcome: Not reviewed. Put the full meeting (each advisor's take, the summary, the recommendation) in the row's page. Write every $ as \$.
- If the agent makes a decision: **Decisions Log** row per the brain rules (Decision, Date, Pillar, Why, Decided By, Still Active, Changes).
- If the agent says something that should guide future advice ("I never want to take on debt"): **AI Memory** row per the brain rules.
- If action items are concrete: offer to add them to **Tasks**.
