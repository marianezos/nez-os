# The fix ladder

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 5. Stop at the first rung that works, and say why the lighter rungs don't. The rung goes in the Fix Type field.

## a. Remove or simplify

Does this task need to exist at all? Can it be shorter, done less often, batched (all CRM updates Friday afternoon), or handled by a template, a checklist or a rule? The best fix often costs nothing. Offer it first.

- **New or solo agents** (few closings last year, or no team in People): lean hardest on this rung. A checklist, a template or batching one afternoon a week usually beats setting up an automation they'll have to maintain.
- **Save it as an SOP:** when the fix is a checklist or a set of steps, offer once: "Want me to save this checklist to your SOPs, so you, a teammate or an AI employee follow the same steps?" No is fine; don't ask again for that fix.

## b. A NEZ OS skill or Dashboard widget you already have (always check first)

Read the live and vault rows in `../../nez-next/references/path.md` check which plugins are installed, and, if /nez-dashboard is installed, the widgets the agent already has. Match the task to what exists, for example:

- Replying to email, a follow-up queue, today's schedule, important dates, tasks, a deal tracker: the /nez-dashboard widgets (once /nez-dashboard is installed).

- Plans, goals, weekly check-ins and reviews: /nez-bizplan, /nez-goals, /nez-review.
- Writing in a person's voice: /nez-voice.
- Answers to objections and client questions: the Objection Bank and Client FAQs from /nez-setup.
- Real estate jobs (daily plan, sphere, listing appointments, listing launch, transactions, seller updates, buyers, prospecting, ads) and marketing and content jobs: the matching skill in nez-real-estate-os, nez-marketing-os or nez-studio-os, or its Vault prompt until the skill ships.

If a skill or widget covers it, point to it, show how to run it for this task, and stop. Don't build anything new.

## c. Platform automation

If their own platform can do it (a GoHighLevel workflow, a calendar rule, an email filter, a form notification, a transaction platform's reminders), give exact steps from `platform-instructions.md`. Never change their platform from here. Anything that sends a message must follow texting consent, A2P, do-not-contact and the NEZ OS messaging rules, and never sends in bulk to people who haven't consented.

## d. AI help

Judgment or writing, but a single job: a prompt to paste, a skill (saved with the save skill card plus a backup file), or a scheduled task that only reads or drafts on a schedule. Build it with `quick-win.md` after the agent's OK. Drafts are never sent without their approval.

## e. An AI employee

Only when the work is a role with several recurring duties that share inputs, tools and one human owner (as a rule of thumb, three or more recurring duties). Follow `ai-employees.md`. If it's really one or two jobs, use rung d.

## f. Keep it human

Say why: it needs judgment, it's a relationship, it's a compliance decision, or they love it. Offer to hand off only the prep or the paperwork around it.

## For each recommendation, show

The task, the rung, why the lighter rungs don't work, what it takes (who builds it, how long, what the agent approves), the estimated hours saved as a range and in dollars ("Estimate, not a promise"), what it costs and the net value (or "cost not known yet"), and what could go wrong.

## Where the time goes

After the recommendations, connect the hours to their plan. Read the time back answer from the interview, and if /nez-goals has a quarter on the Quarterly Goals page, its weekly targets and time check. Say it in one line, for example: "These 5 hours cover the 12 conversations a week your plan is short on." If they have no quarter yet, use Hours per week for finding business and their 90-day goals. Never promise the hours will turn into results.
