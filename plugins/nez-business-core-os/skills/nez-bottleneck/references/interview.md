# Interview

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 3. Scan first and skip anything already known. A few questions at a time, never all at once. Point to what you found ("I see you use GoHighLevel and Gmail, so I won't ask which tools").

## One task

- What's the task, in one sentence? What starts it, and what does "done" look like?
- How long does it take each time, and how many times a week or month? (This gives hours per week. Show the math.)
- Who does it today, and which tools are involved?
- What goes wrong, gets delayed or has to be redone?
- Does it need your judgment or your relationships, or is it the same steps every time?
- What would you do with the time back?

## Full audit (the agent's own work)

One group at a time:

1. **Recurring tasks:** "List the jobs you do every day, week and month: lead follow-up, CRM updates, showings, listing prep, paperwork, content, whatever comes to mind. For each: how long, how often, which tools."
2. **What slips:** "What gets forgotten, delayed or done twice? What causes mistakes or rework? What do clients wait on?"
3. **Drain and joy:** "Which tasks drain you, and which do you love or would never hand off?" Use My personality only as a hint for a better question (if it says they get energy from people, ask whether showings and calls are the energizing work). Never decide for them. Confirm every guess.
4. **Judgment and trust:** "Which tasks need your judgment, a client relationship, or a compliance call, so they should stay with a person?"
5. **The time back:** "What would you do with the hours?" This is often the real reason, and it guides what to fix first. Compare it with Hours per week for finding business and their goals.

## Whole team audit

Offer this copy-and-paste set for each person to answer for themselves, and paste the answers back. Never fill in for a teammate without saying so. Tag every task with its owner's name as it appears in People.

```
Hi [NAME], [AGENT] is looking for what slows the team down so we can fix it. Please list the jobs you do every week (no more than ten). For each one, write what it is, how long it takes each time, how often, and which tools you use. Then answer: what slips or gets redone, which jobs drain you, which jobs you enjoy most, and what you'd do with an extra hour a day. There are no wrong answers, and nothing here is about your performance. Send it back to [AGENT].
```

Say once to the agent: the answers are for finding slow work, and are never used to rate people or to decide about hiring, firing, pay, promotion or discipline.

## AI and tools already in use

Ask what they already use and what it covers (a Claude Project, a scheduled task, GoHighLevel workflows or Conversation AI, a transaction platform, other tools), and check AI Employees, so you never rebuild something that exists.
