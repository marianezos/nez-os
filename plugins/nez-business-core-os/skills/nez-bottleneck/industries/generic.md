# Bottleneck examples for any business

Used by `SKILL.md` when `industry_flow` isn't `real-estate`. Suggestions only, offered after the interview, never assumed. Check what NEZ OS already does before building anything.

## Often slow, and what already exists (check these first)

- **Planning, goals and reviews:** /nez-bizplan, /nez-goals, /nez-review.
- **Writing emails, newsletters, posts, pages and case studies:** the writing skills in nez-marketing-os and nez-studio-os, or their Vault prompts until they ship, in their voice from /nez-voice.
- **Email that needs a reply:** the email widget in /nez-dashboard (once installed), with drafts in their voice.
- **Following up with leads, quotes or past customers:** the follow-up queue widget in /nez-dashboard (once installed), or their CRM's own automation (steps only, consent first).
- **Tasks and important dates:** the Tasks database and, once installed, the /nez-dashboard tasks and dates widgets.
- **Answering the same questions:** Client FAQs and the Objection Bank.
- **Scheduling, reminders and intake forms:** their platform's own automation (steps only).
- **Invoices and payment reminders:** their finance tool's own reminders, or a draft reminder they send themselves.
- **Checklists for repeat jobs:** an SOP in SOPs.

## Possible AI employees (only for several recurring duties, with one human manager)

- **Inbox and follow-up assistant:** sorts the inbox, drafts replies, and builds the follow-up list.
- **Content assistant:** plans and drafts a week of content from their ideas, in their voice.
- **Operations assistant:** keeps checklists, reminders and a weekly summary of what's stuck.
- **Client onboarding assistant:** prepares the welcome material and checklist for each new client.

Each one drafts and never sends, publishes, spends or changes records on its own.

## Often best kept human

Sales conversations, client relationships, pricing decisions, hiring and people decisions, and anything legal, medical, financial or sensitive.
