# Bottleneck examples for real estate

Used by `SKILL.md`. Suggestions only, offered after the interview, never assumed. Real estate rules always apply: Fair Housing (never rank, filter or describe people by who they are), texting consent, A2P and do-not-contact checks, drafts only, compensation set by agreement and never a standard rate, and MLS work only on the agent's own login.

## Often slow, and what already exists (check these first)

For each, use the skill if it's live and installed, otherwise its Vault prompt.

- **Morning planning, who to call, inbox triage:** /nez-daily (nez-real-estate-os) and, once /nez-dashboard is installed, its email and today widgets; /nez-goals for the week's targets.
- **Follow-up that slips:** the follow-up queue widget in /nez-dashboard (once installed), /nez-sphere for past clients and sphere; a GoHighLevel workflow for new-lead response (steps only, consent first).
- **Listing launch content, just listed and just sold posts:** /nez-listing-launch.
- **Listing appointment prep and CMAs:** /nez-listing-appt.
- **Contract to close:** /nez-transaction, and the deal tracker widget in /nez-dashboard (once installed).
- **Seller and buyer updates:** /nez-seller-updates and /nez-buyer.
- **Prospecting expireds, FSBOs and a farm:** /nez-prospect (never automatic; confirm the do-not-call scrub before any call or text).
- **Reviews and referrals:** a plain request through /nez-sphere, and a GoHighLevel review-request workflow (steps only, consent first). Approved reviews go to Proof.
- **Answering the same client questions:** Client FAQs and the Objection Bank (/nez-setup).
- **Ad copy and ad lead follow-up:** /nez-ads (Housing special ad category on Facebook and Instagram).
- **Content and social:** /nez-content and the nez-studio-os skills.

## Possible AI employees (only for several recurring duties, with one human manager)

- **Transaction coordinator assistant:** reads the executed contract, keeps the file checklist and key dates, drafts weekly client updates and reminders. Manager: the agent or the coordinator. Never sends, never changes dates.
- **Lead response and triage assistant:** reads the inbox and new leads read only, sorts what needs a reply, drafts replies in the agent's voice, and builds the day's follow-up list. Manager: the agent. If it lives in GoHighLevel Conversation AI, consent and A2P first, and it hands off anything about price, offers or contracts.
- **Listing marketing assistant:** drafts listing remarks, captions, a content calendar and a graphics brief for each new listing, with a Fair Housing scan. Manager: the agent or the marketing lead.
- **Follow-up and sphere assistant:** drafts the next touch for each person who needs one, with the texting consent check on every text. Manager: the agent.

## Often best kept human

Listing and buyer consultations, pricing conversations, negotiation, offer decisions, client relationships, and anything about compensation, legal or compliance questions.
