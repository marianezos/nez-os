# The voice guide

Used by SKILL.md Step 5. The guide is the heart of the voice: written from the samples and the interview, in plain words, and short enough to use every time (about 600 to 900 words). Use only what the samples and answers show. Never invent a habit.

**First line:** "Voice guide for [Name], version [N], [date]."

## Sections, in this order

1. **Summary.** Three sentences on how this person sounds and what a reader should feel.
2. **Tone.** The feeling (warm, direct, calm, funny...), and what it is not.
3. **Sentence length and rhythm.** Short or long, fragments, questions, how long a paragraph runs.
4. **Vocabulary.** Plain, technical, slang or local words, reading level, and the languages they write in and how they mix them.
5. **Greetings and sign-offs.** How they open and close in each channel, in their exact words.
6. **Signature phrases.** The phrases they say a lot, in their exact words. Rule: no more than one in a piece, and never so it reads like a catchphrase.
7. **Humor.** What kind, how much, and where they keep it out.
8. **Emoji and punctuation.** Which emoji, how many, where; exclamation points, "...", capitals, lowercase habits, dashes. If they use em dashes as a habit, note it and add: "NEZ OS skills still avoid em dashes."
9. **Formality by audience.** One line each: clients, friends, social, team and partners.
10. **Openings and closings.** How they start and end an email, a text, a post, and a video or script.
11. **Stories.** Whether and how they tell stories, how long, and whether the point comes first. Rule: never invent a story; use only stories the agent gives in the task.
12. **Slang and local words.** The words and how often.
13. **Never say.** Words, phrases and moves they'd never use, in their words, plus profanity (yes or no, and where).
14. **Do and don't.** Five to eight short lines each: "Do ..." and "Don't ...".
15. **Channel notes.** One short paragraph each: email, text, social posts and captions, video scripts, long form (blog, newsletter, listing descriptions).
16. **Example lines.** Up to 8 short fragments in their own words that show the voice, each with a one-line note on why it's typical. Replace every phone number, address, email address, account number and client name with a placeholder (`[PHONE]`, `[ADDRESS]`, `[CLIENT NAME]`). Never a full sample.
17. **Samples used.** Descriptions only: kind (email, text, post, transcript), approximate date, where it came from. Never the raw text.

## Rules for writing it

- Use the person's name or "they." Never guess a pronoun.
- Every line must be backed by the samples or the interview. If something's a guess, leave it out or mark it "to confirm" for the test round.
- Style only: no facts about their business, results, clients or personal life.
- Keep it free of private details (the scrub rule in SKILL.md).
- The voice never overrides Compliance Guardrails or Fair Housing; if the person's natural habits break a rule (for example "perfect for families"), list it under Never say.
