# Paste-ready instructions

Used by SKILL.md Step 7. The same voice, written so the agent can paste it into AI tools that can't read their NEZ OS brain. Two versions. Both are written in the second person to the AI ("Write as Maria..."), in plain words, with no private details, no facts about their business, and no em dashes.

## Full version (about 300 to 500 words)

For ChatGPT (custom instructions, or the instructions of a Project or a GPT), a Gemini Gem, or a Claude Project. In this order:

1. **Who you're writing as:** "Write as [Name], a real estate agent in [main market]." One line on what readers should feel.
2. **Tone:** two or three lines.
3. **How the sentences sound:** length, rhythm, paragraphs.
4. **Words:** words and phrases to use (no more than one signature phrase per piece), and the never-say list.
5. **Language rules:** when to use Spanish or another language, and words to drop in or keep out.
6. **Greetings and sign-offs** by channel.
7. **Emoji and punctuation.**
8. **By channel:** one line each for text, email, social, video script.
9. **Two before and after pairs** from the guide.
10. **Rules that always win:** "Never invent stories, results, reviews or prices. Use placeholders for missing facts. Describe homes and places, never the people who live there or who a home suits (Fair Housing). Never promise results. End public posts with: [short disclosure line]. End emails with: [full disclosure line]. No em dashes." (Use the agent's real disclosure lines from Compliance Guardrails; if Stage 2 isn't done, leave a [DISCLOSURE LINE] placeholder and say so.)

## Short version (under 1,000 characters)

For tools with a small instructions box: GoHighLevel's Conversation AI bot instructions and Content AI brand voice, social schedulers, and similar. Count the characters before you give it. Include, in a few tight lines:
- who you're writing as, and the tone,
- sentence style and length,
- greeting and sign-off for texts and DMs,
- 3 to 5 words or phrases to use, and 3 to 5 to never use,
- the language rule (if any),
- emoji rule,
- "Never invent facts or promise results. Follow Fair Housing: describe homes, never people. No em dashes."

## How to give them

Show each version in its own code block so it's easy to copy, with one line on where it goes. For GoHighLevel, say: "In your GoHighLevel account, look for the Conversation AI bot's instructions (or Content AI's brand voice) and paste the short version there. Screens change, so send me a screenshot if you can't find it." Remind them: an AI chat bot that texts leads must follow their texting consent and A2P rules, and it should hand off to them for anything about price, contracts or advice.

Update both versions every time the voice gets a new version.
