# Quarterly Goals page layout

Each quarter lives on the agent's **Quarterly Goals** page (`quarterly_goals_page_id`, inside the Business Plan & Goals page). Never create a database or a second page. Keep older quarters, newest first. Only change the section for the quarter being set or revised.

The page keeps its callout at the top. Quarter sections go below it.

## One section per quarter

A heading 2 with the year, the quarter and its dates, for example **2026 Q4 (Oct 1 to Dec 31)**. If they plan by their own year (a cap year), use their dates. Under it, these parts in this order:

1. **Targets:** a short table, one row per number: closings (or clients), GCI (or revenue), take-home (only if the pay structure or costs are saved), the split by client type or offer, and pending deals already counted.
2. **The math:** the numbered steps from the industry file, each on its own line with their numbers and its label: (your number), (your estimate), (I asked you), (I calculated this) or (assumption: even split).
3. **Timing:** the weeks agreements or proposals have to be signed by, and what's already under contract.
4. **Time check:** hours a week the plan needs vs Hours per week for finding business, and whether it fits.
5. **Priorities:** up to three, taken from the business plan's 90-day roadmap. Three or fewer if their blocker is taking on too much.
6. **Weekly plan:** a table with 13 rows: Week, Dates, Activity targets (by client type), Actions (two or three).
7. **Check-in log:** one line per weekly check-in, newest last: week, date, actuals against plan, status (ahead, on track or behind), any adjustment, and `Sources: Name count, Name count` if they gave sources. Until the first check-in, one line: "No check-ins yet."
8. **Review:** one line, written only by /nez-review: `Latest review: <link> (reviewed <date>)`. When you revise a quarter, keep this line exactly as it is.
9. **Last updated:** today's date and what changed (set, revised, or check-in).

## Rules

- Plain words. The agent should be able to check every line.
- Never remove a past quarter, and never rewrite a check-in or the Review line.
- Commission and income figures stay on this page; never in anything public.
- No em dashes.
