# Goals: real estate

Used by `SKILL.md`. Follow its core rules: use only the pay structure saved by /nez-bizplan, show every step, and never assume listings. Agents work with many kinds of clients, so split every target by the client types they actually serve.

## What to read from My business numbers

- **The goal:** Goal type, Goal number, Plan period, Closings needed, Milestone or award.
- **So far this year:** This year so far: closings, This year so far: GCI.
- **Last year and the funnel:** Last year: closings, Last year: GCI, Leads last year, Agreements signed last year, Closed from those agreements, Average sale price, My commission per side.
- **In progress:** Pending deals now, Active clients likely to close (and Under contract rows in Clients & Deals).
- **The mix:** My business mix, plus Client types I work with (My clients).
- **The pay structure:** Split and cap, Cap year starts and cap paid so far, Fees on each deal, After the cap, Franchise or royalty fee, Monthly or yearly brokerage fees, Team split, Referral fees, Costs outside my brokerage, Team deals count toward my goal, Deal walk-through (confirmed).
- **Time:** Time from first contact to closing, Time from signed agreement to closing (if saved), Hours per week for finding business.

## Questions (only what's missing, once each)

**Client mix.** If My business mix doesn't give a share for every type in Client types I work with (renters, landlords and investors are often missing), ask:
> "Roughly what share of your business is each kind of client? For example 50% buyers, 30% sellers, 20% investors. Rough is fine."
Save it to **My business mix** in their words. Never assume listings or any mix.

**Timing.**
> "From a signed agreement to closing, about how many days does it usually take you?"
Save to **Time from signed agreement to closing**. If it's very different by client type (cash investors vs first-time buyers), ask for each and save them all on the line.

## The chain (every step on its own line)

1. **Yearly target** as they gave it (GCI, take-home or deals) and the period it covers.
2. **Remaining:** minus This year so far (GCI or closings).
3. **This quarter's share:** what remains, split evenly across the quarters left in the plan period unless Seasonality says otherwise (label an even split). Then minus pending deals closing this quarter, each shown.
4. **GCI and take-home,** per deal and for the quarter:
   - Use only the saved pay structure, in the order on Deal walk-through (confirmed). Never use a fee they didn't give, and never fill one in from what you know about a brokerage.
   - Missing line: ask once with the matching /nez-bizplan question (P1 to P11), save it, go on. "Not sure" isn't counted, and you say so in the math.
   - **One deal before the cap:** a short table at the average GCI per deal: GCI, each deduction with what it's taken from and the amount, take-home. Apply any royalty cap using what's been paid so far.
   - **One deal after the cap:** the same deal after capping: after-cap terms, the per-deal fees that continue, take-home.
   - **For the quarter:** walk the deals in order from how much cap is left (Cap year starts, cap paid so far). Say which deal reaches the cap and which reaches the royalty cap. Subtract monthly or yearly fees for the quarter and the outside costs they wanted counted. Show quarter take-home next to the GCI behind it.
   - Goal is take-home: work backward to the GCI needed with the same steps. Goal is GCI: show the take-home it produces.
   - Cap or royalty cap resets inside the quarter: show before and after the reset date, exactly as they described it. Anything unclear: ask.
   - Nothing saved: show GCI only and say: "Take-home needs your split and fees. Run /nez-bizplan to add them and I'll show both."
5. **Deals needed:** quarter GCI ÷ average GCI per deal (from My commission per side × Average sale price, or last year's GCI ÷ last year's closings). Say which you used.
6. **By client type:** split the deals by My business mix, for example "12 deals: 6 buyer, 4 seller, 2 investor." Say how you rounded. If one type's GCI per deal is very different, ask once and use their number.
7. **Work backward for each type:** agreements needed = deals ÷ agreement to close rate. Conversations needed = agreements ÷ lead to agreement rate (or their conversation rates if they have them). Use their overall rates and label them "overall" unless they gave rates by type.
8. **Timing:** closings this quarter come from agreements signed about Time from signed agreement to closing earlier, and agreements signed late in the quarter close next quarter. Show the weeks agreements must be signed by to hit this quarter's closings, and what's already under contract.
9. **Per week:** 13 weeks of targets per client type: conversations, appointments, agreements signed. Name appointments the way each type works: buyer consultations, listing appointments, lease showings, investor meetings.

**Teams:** if Team deals count toward my goal is yes, state targets as team totals and offer per-agent numbers.

**Milestone or award:** show progress toward it using only what they told you, labeled "as you told me." Ask if anything is unclear.

## Headline numbers to save (My business numbers)

Quarter closings goal, Quarter GCI goal, Quarter take-home goal (only if the pay structure is saved), and My business mix if you asked for it.

## Weekly check-in questions

For each client type they serve: conversations, appointments (named for that type), agreements signed, deals that went under contract, and closings. Then any GCI that closed this week. Name everything as it appears in the weekly plan.

## Rules for real estate goals

- Plans describe the agent's own activity. Never describe neighborhoods or who lives there, and never rate an area.
- Never quote a market statistic or industry average as fact.
- Never plan text campaigns to their database without documented texting consent (Compliance Guardrails).
