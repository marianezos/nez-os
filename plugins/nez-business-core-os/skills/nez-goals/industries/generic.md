# Goals: any business

Used by `SKILL.md` when `industry_flow` isn't `real-estate`. Follow its core rules: use only the pricing and costs saved by /nez-bizplan, show every step, and use their own word for what they sell ("clients", "loans", "policies", "projects"). Below, "clients" stands for their word.

## What to read from My business numbers

- **The goal:** Goal type, Goal number, Plan period, Clients needed, Milestone or award.
- **So far this year:** This year so far: clients, This year so far: revenue.
- **Last year and the funnel:** Last year: revenue, Last year: clients, Average value per client, Leads last year, Calls or proposals last year, Became clients.
- **In progress:** Pending deals now, Active clients likely to close.
- **Costs:** Fees on each sale, Payment processing, Partner and referral fees, Monthly or yearly costs, Other costs, What I keep after costs, Deal walk-through (confirmed), Take-home per client.
- **Capacity and time:** My capacity, Time from first contact to closing, Hours per week for finding business.

## Questions (only what's missing, once each)

**Timing.**
> "From a proposal or quote being accepted to the money coming in, about how many days does it usually take?"
Save to **Time from signed agreement to closing** (write "accepted to paid" in the value).

**Offers** (ask once, only if they sell more than one thing):
> "Want to split this quarter's target by what you sell, like by service or product?"
On yes, ask for rough shares and save them to **My business mix** in their words.

## The chain (every step on its own line)

1. **Yearly target** as they gave it (revenue, take-home or clients) and the period it covers.
2. **Remaining:** minus This year so far.
3. **This quarter's share:** what remains, split evenly across the quarters left unless Seasonality says otherwise (label an even split), minus pending clients who pay this quarter.
4. **Revenue and take-home,** per sale and for the quarter:
   - Use only the saved costs, in the order on Deal walk-through (confirmed). Never use a fee they didn't give, and never fill one in from what you know about a platform or processor.
   - Missing line: ask once with the matching /nez-bizplan generic question (P1 to P6), save it, go on. "Not sure" isn't counted.
   - **One sale:** a short table at the average value: revenue, each fee and cost with what it's taken from and the amount, take-home.
   - **For the quarter:** multiply by the clients needed, subtract monthly or yearly costs for the quarter and the other costs they wanted counted, and show take-home next to the revenue behind it.
   - Goal is take-home: work backward to revenue with the same steps. Goal is revenue: show the take-home it produces.
   - Only What I keep after costs saved: use that percent, labeled (your estimate).
   - Nothing saved: show revenue only and say: "Take-home needs your fees and costs. Run /nez-bizplan to add them and I'll show both."
5. **Clients needed:** quarter revenue ÷ Average value per client (or per offer if they split by offer). Say where the average came from.
6. **Work backward:** calls or proposals needed = clients ÷ proposal to client rate. Leads needed = proposals ÷ lead to proposal rate. Their own rates.
7. **Capacity:** compare clients per month with My capacity. If it doesn't fit, say so plainly and offer options: raise prices, add help, change the target, or spread it over more quarters.
8. **Timing:** work that pays this quarter started about Time from signed agreement to closing earlier, and work started late in the quarter pays next quarter. Show the weeks proposals must go out by.
9. **Per week:** 13 weeks of targets: leads or conversations, calls or proposals, clients won.

**Milestone:** show progress using only what they told you, labeled "as you told me."

## Headline numbers to save (My business numbers)

Quarter clients goal, Quarter revenue goal, Quarter take-home goal (only if costs are saved), and My business mix if you asked for it.

## Weekly check-in questions

Leads or conversations, calls or proposals, clients won, and revenue that came in this week.

## Rules

- Targets describe their own activity. No promises about results or income.
- Never quote a benchmark or industry average as fact.
- Follow the advertising and outreach rules their industry has (Compliance Guardrails). Never assume.
