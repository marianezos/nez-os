# First run interview

Used by `SKILL.md` Step 2. Scan first, skip anything the databases and the Master Profile already answer, and point to what you found ("I see Jess listed as your assistant, so I won't ask about her"). A few questions at a time, never all at once.

## 1. The people

- "Who's on your team? Count anyone you'd count: team members, an assistant, a showing agent, an ISA, a contractor you pay for regular work. One line each: name and what they do." The agent is always on the chart.
- **Solo agent:** if they say it's just them, say that's fine: the chart will be them and their AI team, and it's still useful for seeing what AI is working for them and what nobody owns. Go to section 3.
- "Does anyone work part time or only on certain projects?" Note it in their Role text. Don't decide for the agent who counts.
- Partners like lenders, title, inspectors or stagers stay in People but off the tree. If the agent wants them on the chart, show them in a separate "Partners" box, never as reports.
- Never ask personality questions here. For anyone without a profile, mention /nez-self once at the end.

## 2. Who reports to whom

- For each person: "Who do they report to?" Use the exact name as it appears in People. The top person reports to no one.
- If two people could both be the manager, ask which one they go to first. Each person has one manager on the chart.

## 3. AI already doing work

- "Is any AI already doing a job you rely on? For example a Claude Project that drafts your listing descriptions, GoHighLevel Conversation AI answering new leads, a ChatGPT custom GPT, or a scheduled task that writes your weekly update." For each one: what it does, where it runs, and who checks its work.
- **Exactly one human manager:** the person who checks its work and decides what it may do. If the agent says "nobody," say that AI with no owner is how things slip, ask who should own it, and let them choose. If they won't choose, save it as Draft with no manager and flag it.
- **Not every AI tool is an AI employee.** A tool someone uses once in a while (asking Claude a question) isn't one. Offer to leave it off and keep the chart for AI with a recurring job. The agent decides.
- **Texting AI:** if an AI texts leads (like GoHighLevel Conversation AI), note in its What It Does that texting needs A2P approval and documented consent (from Compliance Guardrails). Never say it's compliant.
- Voice skills from /nez-voice are tools, not employees, unless the agent uses one for a recurring job.

## 4. Jobs nobody owns

- "Which jobs come up again and again with no clear owner? For example following up with old leads, posting on social, updating your CRM, or sending past clients a yearly home value." List them as unowned roles. They show on the chart and in the highlights, and /nez-bottleneck can help decide who or what should take them.

## 5. Confirm

Show the proposed rows as two tables (People, AI Employees), with every change labeled new or update. Wait for the agent's OK. Write nothing before it.

- New People row: Name, Role (Team member or Assistant; the job in their words goes in Notes as "Job: ..."), Reports To, Human or AI Human, Status Active.
- New AI Employees row: Name, Role, Human Manager, Where It Runs, What It Does (one line in their words, or blank), Status Draft, Created today.
- Unowned roles: on the agent's own People row under Notes, as "Roles nobody owns: ..." (one line, updated in place).

Say: "The full job description for each AI employee comes from /nez-bottleneck. For now the chart has its name, role and manager."
