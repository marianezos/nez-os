# NEZ OS Setup: Real Estate Flow

The questions for every setup stage, for a real estate agent, team or broker. Ask one at a time. The wording is a guide: say it naturally, adapt to what they already told you, and skip anything already answered. Lines in *italics* are notes for you, not things to say. **Saves to** names the heading and label on the page where each answer goes.

---

# STAGE 1: Core profile (about 10 minutes)

## Group A: You and your market

1. "What name do you use in your business? The one on your signs and social media."
   **Saves to:** Master Profile, About me, Name.
2. "Do you have a business or brand name, or do you go by your own name?"
   **Saves to:** Business or brand name.
3. "Which brokerage are you with?"
   **Saves to:** Brokerage.
4. "How do you run your business?
   A) Solo: I do it all myself
   B) With a partner or spouse
   C) I'm on someone else's team
   D) I lead my own team
   E) I own the brokerage"
   **Saves to:** How I run my business.
   *Then ask the matching follow-up and save both answers on one line under Team details:*
   - *Partner or spouse: "Is your brand shared or separate, and do you create content together?"*
   - *On a team: "Do you market under the team brand, your own, or both?"*
   - *Team leader: "How many agents are on your team, and are you still selling yourself?"*
   - *Broker/owner: "How many agents are in your office, and are you still selling or focused on leading?"*
5. "How many years have you been licensed?"
   **Saves to:** Years licensed.
6. "Which state or states are you licensed in?"
   **Saves to:** States I'm licensed in.
7. "What languages do you work in?"
   **Saves to:** Languages.
8. "What city or area is your main market?"
   **Saves to:** My market, Main market.
9. "Any surrounding towns, counties or neighborhoods you also serve?"
   **Saves to:** Surrounding areas I also serve.
10. "What did you do before real estate?" *(Experience from another career is often their best story.)*
    **Saves to:** About me, What I did before real estate.
11. "Why did you get into real estate? The real reason, not the bio version."
    **Saves to:** Why I got into real estate.

→ **Save Group A.** Setup Status: `stage_1_core_profile: in progress: group A done`.

## Group B: Your tools and where you show up

1. "Do you have a website? If yes, what's the address?"
   **Saves to:** My brand, Website.
2. "What CRM do you use for your contacts and leads?
   A) GoHighLevel
   B) Follow Up Boss
   C) kvCORE
   D) Another CRM (which one?)
   E) A spreadsheet
   F) None yet"
   **Saves to:** My tools, CRM.
3. *(Skip if F.)* "How much are you really using it?
   A) Barely: it mostly holds contacts
   B) Basics: contacts and some notes
   C) Pipelines and some automations
   D) All in: it runs my follow-up"
   **Saves to:** How I use my CRM.
4. "What do you use for booking appointments?
   A) My CRM's calendar
   B) Calendly
   C) Google Calendar
   D) Something else
   E) I schedule by hand"
   **Saves to:** Calendar or scheduling.
5. "Which AI tools do you use? (Claude, ChatGPT, others.)"
   **Saves to:** AI tools.
6. "Which social platforms are you active on? Share the links." *(Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, an email list. Accept several.)*
   **Saves to:** My brand, Social profiles.
7. *(Skip if they gave a YouTube link.)* "Do you have a YouTube channel?
   A) Yes (link?)
   B) Not yet, but I want one
   C) Not planning to"
   **Saves to:** YouTube.
8. "Anything else you use every week? Design, video, email, transactions, e-signatures." *(Canva, CapCut, Dotloop, and so on.)*
   **Saves to:** Software Stack: one row per tool from questions 2, 4, 5 and 8 (Tool, Category, Used For). Check "Connected to AI" only if they say it's connected to their AI. Master Profile, Everything else: "see Software Stack."
9. "What hours do you work, and which hours do you protect for family or yourself?"
   **Saves to:** How I work, Hours I work and hours I protect.
10. "What's the best way for clients to reach you: text, call or email?"
    **Saves to:** Best way for clients to reach me.
11. "How do you want your AI to work with you? For example: always give me options, ask before you assume, keep it short."
    **Saves to:** My boundaries, How I want AI to work with me.
12. "Anything you don't do? Services, tasks or kinds of deals you'd rather refer out."
    **Saves to:** Things I don't do.
13. "Any situations you don't take on? Describe the situation, like 'rentals under 6 months' or 'out-of-state listings.'" *(Fair Housing: if they describe people, reword it to the situation with them.)*
    **Saves to:** Clients or situations I don't take on.
14. "When are you off limits? Days or hours your AI should never schedule or promise for you."
    **Saves to:** When I'm off limits.
15. "Anything your AI should never say or promise on your behalf?"
    **Saves to:** Never say or promise for me.

*Never ask for or save passwords or logins.*

→ **Save Group B.** Setup Status: `in progress: group B done`.

## Group C: Your business and your clients

Say first: "Now a few questions about your business. This is what lets me write and plan like someone who knows your market. Say 'skip' on anything you'd rather answer later."

1. "What's the typical price range you work in?"
   **Saves to:** My market, Typical price range.
2. "In your own words, what's your market doing right now? Prices, inventory, how fast homes sell. Rough is fine."
   **Saves to:** What my market is doing right now. *(Their impression, not a stat. Don't add numbers.)*
3. "If a friend moving to your area asked what makes it special, what would you tell them?" *(Places, lifestyle, commute, things to do. If they describe people, reword to describe the place and explain why in one sentence.)*
   **Saves to:** What makes my area special.
4. "Do you mostly work with buyers, sellers, or both?
   A) Mostly buyers
   B) Mostly sellers
   C) About even"
   **Saves to:** My clients, Mostly buyers, sellers or both.
5. "Which kinds of clients do you work with? Pick all that fit:
   Sellers · Buyers · First-time buyers · Investors · Landlords · Renters · Luxury · New construction · Relocation · 55+ communities · Commercial · Land · Other"
   **Saves to:** Client types I work with (a list, in their order). *Every skill reads this list, and Stage 5 builds the Objection Bank from it, so make sure it's complete.*
6. *(Skip if they picked one type.)* "Is one of those your specialty, or do you work with everyone about the same?"
   **Saves to:** My specialty (their words, or "general").
7. "What usually makes clients reach out to you? What's going on in their life right then?" *(Situations, never people.)*
   **Saves to:** What makes them reach out for help.
8. "When clients first talk to you, what are they usually most worried about?"
   **Saves to:** Their biggest worries.
9. "What do they need to hear from you to feel confident?"
   **Saves to:** What they need to hear from me.
10. "What are the 3 to 5 questions clients ask you most? Give me your usual answer for each, even if it's rough."
   **Saves to:** **Client FAQs**: one row per question (Client Type, My Answer in their words, a one-line Short Version you write and they approve, Status Approved once they OK it, Last Updated today). Never invent an answer; if they're unsure, Status Draft.
11. "Rank where your business comes from, biggest first: sphere and referrals, past clients, open houses, social media, YouTube, paid ads, online leads (Zillow, Realtor.com and so on), door knocking or cold calls, partners, or something else."
   **Saves to:** Where my business comes from (an ordered list).
12. "Walk me through working with you as a **buyer**, from first call to keys."
    **Saves to:** Offers & Services, For buyers (What's included, My buyer process).
13. "And as a **seller**: what's in your marketing, and what are the main steps?"
    **Saves to:** Offers & Services, For sellers (My listing marketing plan, What's included, My seller process).
14. "Any programs you know really well? First-time buyer programs, down payment assistance, VA, FHA, and so on."
    **Saves to:** Offers & Services, For buyers, Special programs I know well (or Other clients I serve if it's for investors, renters or relocation).
15. "Do you offer any free guides, tools, classes or events?"
    **Saves to:** Offers & Services, Free resources I offer (each with its link) and Classes, events or workshops.

*Never write promises or guarantees into Offers & Services. If they say "I'll sell your home in 30 days," save it as their goal or process, and say why in one sentence.*

→ **Save Group C.** Setup Status: `in progress: group C done`.

## Group D: Your brand (optional)

Say first: "Last group, and it's optional. Say 'skip' on anything and we'll come back to it."

1. "Where does your branding stand?
   A) Fully branded and consistent everywhere
   B) A logo, but things don't match across platforms
   C) It needs a refresh
   D) Starting from scratch"
   **Saves to:** My brand, Where my branding stands.
2. "Your brand name(s) and tagline, if you have them?"
   **Saves to:** Brand name(s) and tagline.
3. "Your main brand colors? Hex codes are great if you have them; 'navy and gold' works too."
   **Saves to:** Colors.
4. "Any fonts you use in your marketing? It's fine if you're not sure."
   **Saves to:** Fonts.
5. **Services offer** *(ask at most once ever: skip it if `services_offer_asked` in Setup Status has any value).* First ask: "Do you already have someone who handles your CRM, website and marketing systems for you?" If yes, write "no" under Interested in branding or systems help, set `services_offer_asked: yes`, and move on. If no, pick the one or two services that fit their answers best, lead with them, and mention the rest in one line:
   - CRM answered "barely" or "basics", or no CRM yet → **GoHighLevel setup:** pipelines, automations and follow-up built for them.
   - Uses GoHighLevel or wants to text their database → **Texting approval (A2P):** registration and website fixes so texts get approved the first time.
   - No website, or branding is B, C or D → **Website or area guide site.**
   - Lead sources are mostly sphere or referrals, or they want more online leads → **Lead magnet funnel** with comment-to-DM delivery.
   - Felt setup was a lot, or asks for help → **Done-for-you NEZ OS setup:** we fill in your brain with you.

   Then say, for example:
   > "If you ever want this done for you, the Maria Nez AI team builds [the one or two that fit] for agents. We also do [the others, in a few words]. Ask in the NEZ OS community and we'll send you the options. Want me to note what you're interested in?
   > A) Yes, note it
   > B) No thanks"
   **Saves to:** Interested in branding or systems help: the services they want noted, or "no". Set `services_offer_asked: yes` either way. Never repeat the offer in later stages or skills.

→ **Save Group D.** Setup Status: `stage_1_core_profile: <today's date>`. Stage 1 is done.

# STAGE 2: Compliance (about 5 minutes)

Template: `templates/compliance.md`.

Intro: "This sets the rules every draft follows: Fair Housing, advertising rules and your disclosure line. Once it's done, what I write for you is ready to use. A few quick questions about your brokerage and state."

1. "You told me your brokerage is [brokerage]. Is that exactly how it appears on your license and ads? If not, give me the exact wording." *(If brokerage is blank, ask for the licensed name.)*
2. "What's your license number? If your state also wants a broker or team license number in ads, include that too."
3. "How do you show Equal Housing Opportunity in your marketing? Most agents use the words 'Equal Housing Opportunity,' often with the logo. Tell me your wording, or say 'standard.'"
4. "Does your state or brokerage require any other wording in ads? For example the REALTOR® mark, your team name next to the brokerage, or where you're licensed. Say 'none' or 'not sure'; we can add it later."

   *Put 1 to 4 together into one full disclosure line, for example: "[Name], [Brokerage], License #[number]. Equal Housing Opportunity. [Other required text]." Show it and ask if that's exactly what they want at the end of emails and web pages.*
   **Saves to:** Compliance Guardrails, My details, Full disclosure line.
5. "Do you use a shorter version for captions, ads and video descriptions, where the full line won't fit? Check with your broker that a short version is allowed where you'll use it. If you don't have one, I'll use the full line everywhere."
   **Saves to:** Short disclosure line (the full line if none).
6. "Does your state or brokerage require certain notices or links in some messages? For example, some states have a brokerage services disclosure or a consumer notice that agents link in emails or on their website. For each one, tell me its name, the link and where you use it. Please confirm with your broker where each is required; I won't guess. Say 'none' or 'not sure.'"
   **Saves to:** Required notices (one per line: name | link | where I use it). *Never tell them a notice is or isn't required by law. Use only what they give you.*
7. "Fair Housing protects certain groups everywhere in the US, and many states and cities protect more. I have you licensed in [states] with your main market in [main market]. Which states and cities should I follow so I avoid every protected group there? Please confirm the list with your broker or attorney."
   **Saves to:** Fair Housing areas to follow. *Suggest the states and main city from their profile, then confirm.*
8. "Does your brokerage have advertising rules I should follow? Things like team name rules, broker approval before ads go live, or 'coming soon' rules. Say 'none' if not."
   **Saves to:** Brokerage advertising rules.
9. "Do you have documented permission, like a signed form or an online opt-in, to text the people in your database?
   A) Yes
   B) No
   C) Not sure"
   **Saves to:** Texting consent for my database. *If B or C, say: "No problem. I'll keep text campaigns off the table for now, and I can show you how to collect permission." If they use GoHighLevel, mention their texting registration (A2P) has to be approved before campaigns run.*
10. "Any other rule from your state, MLS or brokerage I should always follow?"
    **Saves to:** My state and brokerage rules.

Then fill the page per the template, set Last confirmed to today, show the five-point summary from the template, and ask: "Want me to follow these on everything I draft for you?" Tell them: "You're still responsible for final compliance, so double-check anything new with your broker."

Setup Status: `stage_2_compliance: <today's date>`.

# STAGE 3: Voice and edge (about 8 minutes)

Template: `templates/voice.md`.

Intro: "This is what makes my writing sound like you instead of any agent: who you're best for, what sets you apart, and how you actually talk. A few questions, then I'll write a short voice profile for you to approve."

## Part 1: Your edge

1. "What are the 2 or 3 client situations you handle best? Describe the situation, not the person. For example 'sellers who have to sell before they can buy' or 'buyers relocating on a deadline.'"
   **Saves to:** Master Profile, My edge, Client situations I handle best. *If they describe people by a protected group (age, family status, religion and so on), reword it into the situation with them: the timeline, the goal or the challenge.*
2. "What do you do that other agents in your market don't? Give me one proof point too: a number, a result, or a step in your process."
   **Saves to:** What I do that other agents don't, and Proof point. *No proof? Ask once: "Any number, result or story that backs that up?" Still none: write `[ADD PROOF]`.*
3. *(Only if they gave proof.)* "Is it OK to use that in public marketing, or keep it for conversations only? Some agents don't want dollar amounts in public posts."
   **Saves to:** OK to use the proof point publicly (yes / no).
4. "What three words would your best clients use to describe working with you?"
   **Saves to:** Three words clients use for me.
5. "Tell me your story in a few sentences: where you started, a turning point, and why you do this now."
   **Saves to:** My story in 3 sentences, and a **Stories** row (Type Origin) with the full story in their words on its page.
6. "Tell me one client win you're proud of, and one lesson the business taught you. Leave out client names." *(Skippable. One at a time.)*
   **Saves to:** **Stories**: one row each (Type Client win or Lesson), the story in their words on the page.
7. "What do you believe about buying or selling a home that other agents might not say out loud?"
   **Saves to:** **Stories** (Type Belief).
   *For every story, ask: "OK to use this publicly, or conversations only?" and set OK to Share. Ask what it's best for (content, listing appointments, buyer consults, ads, bio, follow-up) and set Best For. Remove any client name, address or number.*

→ **Save Part 1.**

## Part 2: Your voice

1. "Any words, phrases or habits you never want in your writing? Things you'd never say, or that sound fake to you."
   **Saves to:** Voice Library, I never sound like this.
2. "How formal is your writing?
   A) Casual and conversational
   B) Professional and polished
   C) In between"
3. "Your sentences are usually:
   A) Short and punchy
   B) Long and flowing
   C) A mix"
4. "Emojis?
   A) Never
   B) Sometimes
   C) Often"
5. "What length do you like for texts, captions and emails?
   A) Short: get to the point
   B) Medium
   C) Long and detailed"
6. *(Skip if they work in one language.)* "When do you mix in Spanish (or your other language), and how?"
7. *(Skippable.)* "Any phrases you say all the time, on calls or in your content?"
   **Saves to:** Words and phrases I use.
8. *(Skippable.)* "In a few words, how do you want to come across? For example calm expert, friendly neighbor, straight shooter."
9. "Should I avoid em dashes (the long dash that joins two thoughts) in everything I write for you? A lot of readers think they make writing sound like AI.
   A) Yes, avoid them (recommended)
   B) They're fine"
   *Skipped counts as A.*

**Saves to:** Voice Library, How I sound and My voice in a few words (questions 2 to 6, 8 and 9).

→ **Save Part 2.**

## Part 3: Writing samples (optional, and worth it)

"If you'd like, paste 3 to 5 things you actually wrote: texts to clients, emails, captions or your bio. A mix of casual and professional is best. I'll use them to match your voice closely. Say 'skip' to finish without them."

If they paste samples, follow the Samples section of `templates/voice.md`: write the voice summary, three sample messages and three gaps, check each sample against their originals, and revise until they say "that sounds like me." Save their originals under My real samples.

Setup Status: `stage_3_voice_and_edge: <today's date>` and `voice_profile: <today's date>` (only if they approved a voice summary).

# STAGE 4: Goals (about 8 minutes)

Intro: "Now we turn your goal into numbers you can actually track: closings, appointments and conversations per week."

1. "What's your goal for the next 12 months, in your own words? Deals, income, or a life goal."
   **Saves to:** Master Profile, My goals this year, My 12-month goal in my words.
2. "And your top 3 goals for the next 90 days? Tell me why each one matters."
   **Saves to:** My top 3 goals for the next 90 days, and why.
3. "What does a good month look like for you? Closings, appointments, time off, how you feel."
   **Saves to:** What a good month looks like.
4. "What's your gross commission income goal for this year?"
5. "Roughly what's your average sale price?"
6. "Your average commission per side, as a percent or dollars?"
7. "Your split and fees: brokerage split, cap, transaction fees?"
8. "Do you know your conversion rates, like how many conversations it takes to get an appointment?" *(If not, use the estimates below and label them.)*
9. "What do you want more of this year?"
10. "And less of?"
11. "What are your top 3 priorities this quarter?" *(Skip if the 90-day goals already answer it; copy them into This quarter.)*

**Do the math in the chat, step by step,** so they can check it:
- Net per closing = average price × commission per side × (1 − split) − per-deal fees
- Closings needed = income goal ÷ net per closing (round up)
- Work backward to contracts, appointments, conversations and leads per year, month and week.
- Starting estimates when they don't know theirs (label them as estimates): 1 closing per 1.2 contracts, 1 contract per 3 appointments, 1 appointment per 10 conversations, 1 conversation per 5 new leads.

**Saves to:** Master Profile, My goals this year (income goal, closings, more of, less of). Business Plan & Goals: The big picture, The math table, Top lead sources (from Stage 1 Group C), This quarter.

Setup Status: `stage_4_goals: <today's date>`.

# STAGE 5: Objection bank (about 10 minutes per client type)

Template: `templates/objection-bank.md`. Starter objections: `templates/objections/`.

First check Stage 3. If it isn't done, say: "This works best after your voice and edge, because I'll write the answers in your voice with your proof. Do that first, or go ahead with a general voice?" Follow their choice.

Intro: "Clients push back in predictable ways, and the best answer depends on who you're talking to. I have starter answers for the most common pushback from each type of client. I'll rewrite them in your voice with your real proof, one client type at a time, so you have answers ready for calls, texts and follow-up."

## Step 1: Confirm the client types
Read Client types I work with. Say: "You told me you work with [list]. Still right? Add or remove any." If the list is blank, ask Stage 1 Group C question 5 once and save it. Then ask which type to start with, and suggest the one that's most of their business.

## Step 2: Proof (ask once, for all types)
"What proof can I use in your answers? Results, numbers, steps in your process, or client reviews. Paste whatever you have. I won't make anything up; where there's no proof I'll mark it [ADD PROOF] so you can fill it in later."
Start from the proof point in My edge and ask "Anything to add?" For each new one, ask if it's OK in public or conversations only, and remember which. Answers in the Objection Bank are for conversations, so private proof is fine there, but mark it "(private)."

## Step 3: One client type at a time
Follow the "Building a client type" steps in `templates/objection-bank.md`:
1. Read the starter file for that type and tell them which objections it covers.
2. Ask: "What pushback do you hear most from [client type]? Add as many as you like, or say 'none.'"
3. Write every starter and every one they added, in their voice, with one proof point each (or [ADD PROOF]). Run the template's final check.
4. Show each answer and ask for changes.
5. Save one Objection Bank row per objection. Setup Status: `stage_5_objection_bank: in progress: [types done]`.
6. Ask: "Want to do [next type] now, or stop here?" Never push.

When every chosen type is done, set `stage_5_objection_bank: <today's date>` and say: "Your Objection Bank is saved in your brain. The Conversion Coach on your Advisory Board reads it, and you can add to it any time with /nez-setup. When an answer works on a real call, mark it Works."

# STAGE 6: Market areas (about 10 minutes per area)

Template: `templates/market-area.md`. Repeat for each area. Two or three is plenty for a first pass.

Intro: "A fact file for each area gives me real local detail for your listings, posts and emails. It covers the place, the homes and what's nearby, never who lives there. Twelve short questions. Which area should we start with?"

*Check Market Areas first. If the area already has a row, ask "Replace it or add to it?"*

0. "Is it a city, neighborhood, subdivision or zip code?"

## Group 1: The homes
1. "What's the typical price range there, and the most common home styles and lot sizes?"
2. "How fast are homes selling lately, and how does inventory feel? Share any numbers you can back up, with where they're from and when."
3. "Is there an HOA? Roughly what does it cost, what does it cover, and which rules come up?"

## Group 2: Living there
4. "Which parks, trails, restaurants and shopping do you point people to?"
5. "How do people get around? Main roads, highway or train access, and typical drive times to downtown or big employers."
6. "What events or seasonal traditions are part of life there?"

## Group 3: What you know that others don't
7. "Which streets or sections are best for certain features, like bigger yards, mature trees, flat lots or walkability?"
8. "What surprises people who are new to the area?"
9. "Any seasonal quirks you've seen yourself, like traffic, standing water after big storms, or busy times of year?" *(The file will tell readers to check anything like this with the official source.)*

## Group 4: For your clients
10. "What kinds of homes sell fastest there, and what do they have in common?"
11. "What inspection or maintenance issues come up a lot with homes there?"
12. "What should your clients know or ask before they buy, sell, rent or invest there?"

*Ask nothing about who lives there. If they describe residents, school quality or safety, say kindly: "I'll leave that out, since it can come across as steering under Fair Housing. Let's stick to the place, the homes and what's nearby." and move on.*

Then build and save the area per the template, show the snapshot and content ideas, and ask if it looks right. Setup Status: `stage_6_market_areas: <today's date> (N areas)`. Ask: "Add another area now, or stop here?"
