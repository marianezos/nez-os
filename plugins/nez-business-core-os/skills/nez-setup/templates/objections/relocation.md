# Starter objections: Relocation

NEZ OS starters for Stage 5. Rewrite each answer in the agent's voice, swap [PROOF] for one of their proof points (or [ADD PROOF]), and add the pushback they hear most. Describe areas by places, commute and features, never by who lives there. Point to official sources for schools and safety.

## 1. "We'll rent first and figure out the area."
- **Category:** Timing
- **Behind it:** Fear of buying in the wrong part of town.
- **Ask first:** "What would you need to know about an area to feel sure about it?"
- **Answer idea:** Renting first can be smart. Offer to help either way: a short tour of areas by commute, home styles and what's nearby, so they learn faster. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Renting first can be smart. Want a quick video tour of the areas that fit your commute, so you can narrow it down?
- **Don't say:** Which areas are "safe," "good" or full of a certain kind of people.

## 2. "We can't come see homes in person."
- **Category:** Trust & process
- **Behind it:** Worry about buying something they haven't seen.
- **Ask first:** "What would you need to see to feel comfortable from far away?"
- **Answer idea:** Explain how you show homes remotely: live video tours, honest notes on what you see and smell and hear, and inspections. [PROOF]
- **Text:** I can be your eyes. Want a live video tour of the next home, so you can ask anything as we walk it?
- **Don't say:** That a remote purchase has no risks.

## 3. "Our company already gave us an agent."
- **Category:** Other agent
- **Behind it:** They think they have to use the company's agent.
- **Ask first:** "Do you know if your relocation package lets you choose your agent?"
- **Answer idea:** Some packages require a specific agent and some don't. Suggest they check, and offer local knowledge either way. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Worth checking your package. If you can choose, I'd love to help, and I'm happy to answer area questions either way.
- **Don't say:** Anything negative about the company's agent.

## 4. "We have no idea what things cost here."
- **Category:** Market
- **Behind it:** Sticker shock or uncertainty.
- **Ask first:** "What did a similar home cost where you live now?"
- **Answer idea:** Offer a simple comparison: what their current kind of home costs in each area here, with sources and dates. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Send me what you have now and I'll show you what that looks like here, area by area.
- **Don't say:** Numbers without a source and date.

## 5. "We need to sell our current home first."
- **Category:** Timing
- **Behind it:** Fear of owning two homes.
- **Ask first:** "What would make the timing feel safe?"
- **Answer idea:** Lay out the options for timing the sale and purchase, and connect them with an agent where they live now and a lender here. [PROOF]
- **Text:** There are ways to time it so you're not stuck. Want me to walk you through them?
- **Don't say:** Lending or timing promises.

## 6. "Our timeline is too tight."
- **Category:** Timing
- **Behind it:** The move date is fixed and close.
- **Ask first:** "What's the latest date you need to be in a home?"
- **Answer idea:** Build a plan backward from their move date, line up a lender and your remote tour process now, and have a backup like a short-term rental. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Let's plan backward from your move date. Can we do a quick call today?
- **Don't say:** Promises that it'll all work out on time.

## 7. "We're worried about costs and taxes in the new area."
- **Category:** Money & readiness
- **Behind it:** They don't know the real cost of living there.
- **Ask first:** "What costs matter most to you: taxes, HOA, insurance, commute?"
- **Answer idea:** Share property tax, HOA and insurance information from official sources with dates, and point tax questions to a CPA. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Let's compare real costs area by area. Want me to put it together?
- **Don't say:** Numbers without a source and date. Tax advice.
