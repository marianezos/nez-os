# Starter objections: Luxury

NEZ OS starters for Stage 5. Rewrite each answer in the agent's voice, swap [PROOF] for one of their proof points (or [ADD PROOF]), and add the pushback they hear most. Respect privacy, and never share one client's details with another. Describe homes and features, never the people who live nearby.

## 1. "You haven't sold many homes at this price."
- **Category:** Trust & process
- **Behind it:** They want proof you can reach the right buyers and negotiate at this level.
- **Ask first:** "What would you need to see to feel confident in how your home is marketed?"
- **Answer idea:** Be honest about your experience. Show the specific plan: presentation, where the home will be seen, how buyers are qualified before showings, and who's on your team. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Fair question. Can I walk you through the exact plan for your home, step by step?
- **Don't say:** Numbers you can't back up. Anything about other clients' homes or prices.

## 2. "We want it marketed privately."
- **Category:** Trust & process
- **Behind it:** Privacy, or worry about looking like they're struggling to sell.
- **Ask first:** "What matters most: who sees it, what they see, or how many people come through?"
- **Answer idea:** Explain the options and trade-offs honestly, including any MLS and brokerage rules about private listings. [PROOF] Agree on a plan that protects privacy and still reaches qualified buyers.
- **Text:** We can protect your privacy. Want to go over the options and what each one means for reaching buyers?
- **Don't say:** Anything that breaks MLS or brokerage rules.

## 3. "We'll test a high price and see."
- **Category:** Price
- **Behind it:** Few comparable sales, so they hope the right buyer will pay more.
- **Ask first:** "How long are you willing to test it before we look at the price again?"
- **Answer idea:** With few sales to compare, pricing is a strategy. Agree on a starting price backed by the closest sales, a review date and what will trigger a change. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Let's agree on a starting price and a review date, so we're in control of the plan. Free to talk this week?
- **Don't say:** "It'll sit forever." Guarantees about price or timing.

## 4. "We already know the buyers in our circle."
- **Category:** Other agent
- **Behind it:** They think their network is enough.
- **Ask first:** "Has anyone in your circle been serious about buying it?"
- **Answer idea:** Their network matters, and you'll include it. Explain how you also reach buyers outside it so they get the best possible offer. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Your network is a real asset. Want me to show you how we'd reach buyers beyond it too?
- **Don't say:** Anything dismissive about their connections.

## 5. "Our home is one of a kind, so the comps don't apply."
- **Category:** Price
- **Behind it:** They believe the home's features put it in a class of its own.
- **Ask first:** "Which features do you think buyers will value most?"
- **Answer idea:** Agree that unique homes need more than comps. Show the closest sales, what buyers at that level paid for similar features, and a pricing plan with a review date. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Let's look at the closest sales and what buyers paid for features like yours. Free this week?
- **Don't say:** 'It's just like the others.' Guarantees on price.

## 6. "Your fee should be lower on a higher-priced home."
- **Category:** Commission & fees
- **Behind it:** They expect the percentage to shrink as the price grows.
- **Ask first:** "What would you need to see to feel the fee is earned?"
- **Answer idea:** Walk through what the plan includes at this level (presentation, reach, screening buyers, negotiation) and what's at stake in the final price. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Fair to ask. Want a one-page breakdown of what's included for your home?
- **Don't say:** 'All fees are standard.' Cutting your fee on the spot.

## 7. "We want the biggest-name agent or brokerage."
- **Category:** Other agent
- **Behind it:** They equate a big name with better results.
- **Ask first:** "What would the biggest name do for you that matters most?"
- **Answer idea:** Focus on the specific plan and attention they'd get from you, and the reach your brokerage and marketing provide. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Totally fair. Can I show you the exact plan I'd run for your home?
- **Don't say:** Anything negative about other agents or brokerages.
