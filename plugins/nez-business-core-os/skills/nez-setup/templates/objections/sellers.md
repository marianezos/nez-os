# Starter objections: Sellers

NEZ OS starters for Stage 5. Rewrite each answer in the agent's voice, swap [PROOF] for one of their proof points (or [ADD PROOF]), and add the pushback they hear most. Use only facts the agent gave you. Never promise a price or a sale date.

## 1. "Another agent will charge me less."
- **Category:** Commission & fees
- **Behind it:** They can see the fee, but not yet what they get for it.
- **Ask first:** "Besides the fee, what would make you feel the sale went really well?"
- **Answer idea:** Agree that comparing fees is smart. Shift the comparison to what each plan includes and what the seller walks away with at closing: pricing, prep, marketing, how offers and repairs get negotiated. [PROOF] Offer a side-by-side of your plan so they can compare apples to apples.
- **Text:** Smart to compare. Want me to send a one-page breakdown of exactly what's included so you can compare plans side by side?
- **Don't say:** "You get what you pay for." Anything about the other agent. "My fee is standard." Don't drop your fee on the spot.

## 2. "We'll wait until the market gets better."
- **Category:** Timing
- **Behind it:** Fear of selling at the wrong moment, or hope for a bigger number.
- **Ask first:** "What would need to change for it to feel like the right time?"
- **Answer idea:** Respect that it's their call. Nobody can time the market, so focus on what can be measured: what similar homes sell for now, how long they take, and what it costs to hold the home while waiting. [PROOF] Offer to lay out selling now versus later in numbers.
- **Text:** Totally your call. Want a quick side-by-side of selling now versus waiting, so the decision is based on numbers?
- **Don't say:** Predictions about prices or rates. "You'll lose money if you wait." Any urgency you can't back up.

## 3. "We want to list higher than your number."
- **Category:** Price
- **Behind it:** Attachment to the home, a number they need for their next move, or belief that upgrades add more.
- **Ask first:** "What number makes your next step work, and what tells you the home is worth that?"
- **Answer idea:** Show you heard their number. Explain that buyers compare the home to what's for sale and what sold nearby, and that the first weeks get the most attention. [PROOF] Walk the comps together and agree on a plan: list where the data supports, with a set date to review.
- **Text:** Let's look at the comps together so we're seeing the same picture. Does tonight or tomorrow work?
- **Don't say:** "Your home isn't worth that." "It'll never sell at that price." Any guarantee on price or timing.

## 4. "We're going to sell it ourselves."
- **Category:** Trust & process
- **Behind it:** They want to save money and believe they can handle it.
- **Ask first:** "Which parts feel easy to you, and which part feels like the biggest unknown?"
- **Answer idea:** Respect the choice. Name what usually surprises owners: pricing, screening buyers, negotiating after inspection, and the paperwork. [PROOF] Offer a free pricing review so they start from solid numbers, and leave the door open.
- **Text:** Happy to help either way. Want a free pricing review so you start from solid numbers? No strings attached.
- **Don't say:** "Sale-by-owner homes never sell." "You'll leave money on the table." Anything that sounds like a threat.

## 5. "We're not ready yet."
- **Category:** Timing
- **Behind it:** They fear pressure, or they're still gathering information.
- **Ask first:** "What would 'ready' look like for you?"
- **Answer idea:** Take the pressure off out loud. Many owners like to know their options early so they're prepared. [PROOF] Offer a no-obligation read on where the home stands and what to fix first, and check back only if they want you to.
- **Text:** No rush at all. Want a quick idea of where your home stands today, so you have it when you're ready?
- **Don't say:** "You need to act now." Repeated follow-ups after a clear no.
