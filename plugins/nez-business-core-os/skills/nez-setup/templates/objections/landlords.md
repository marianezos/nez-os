# Starter objections: Landlords

NEZ OS starters for Stage 5. Rewrite each answer in the agent's voice, swap [PROOF] for one of their proof points (or [ADD PROOF]), and add the pushback they hear most. Tenant screening must follow fair housing law; never suggest screening anyone by a protected class. Legal questions go to their attorney.

## 1. "I want more rent than you're suggesting."
- **Category:** Price
- **Behind it:** They need to cover costs, or they think upgrades justify more.
- **Ask first:** "What rent do you need to cover your costs, and what makes you feel it's worth more?"
- **Answer idea:** Show what similar places rent for and how long they sit empty. An empty month costs more than a slightly lower rent. [PROOF] Agree on a starting rent and a date to review.
- **Text:** Let's look at what similar places are renting for, so we price it to fill fast. Want me to send them?
- **Don't say:** "It'll never rent at that." Guarantees on rent or vacancy.

## 2. "I'll manage it myself."
- **Category:** Trust & process
- **Behind it:** They want to save the fee and keep control.
- **Ask first:** "Which part of managing it do you enjoy, and which part do you dread?"
- **Answer idea:** Respect the choice. Offer help with the parts that are hardest to do alone: pricing, marketing, screening that follows the rules, and the lease. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Totally fine. Want help with just the leasing part so you start with a solid tenant and lease?
- **Don't say:** Anything that sounds like they can't handle it.

## 3. "I've had bad tenants before."
- **Category:** Trust & process
- **Behind it:** A bad experience they don't want to repeat.
- **Ask first:** "What happened, and what would have prevented it?"
- **Answer idea:** Explain your screening process: consistent written criteria applied the same way to every applicant, plus a clear lease. [PROOF]
- **Text:** I'm sorry that happened. Want me to walk you through how I screen every applicant the same way?
- **Don't say:** Anything about screening out types of people. Promises that a tenant will never cause problems.

## 4. "Should I just sell instead?"
- **Category:** Market
- **Behind it:** They're tired of landlording or worried about the market.
- **Ask first:** "What would make keeping it worth it to you?"
- **Answer idea:** Offer to lay out both paths side by side: what selling could bring and what renting brings each year, with their CPA weighing in on taxes. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Good question to ask. Want a side-by-side of selling versus renting so you can talk it over with your CPA?
- **Don't say:** Tax advice. Predictions about rents or prices.

## 5. "Property managers charge too much."
- **Category:** Commission & fees
- **Behind it:** They see the fee but not the time and risk it covers.
- **Ask first:** "What's your time worth on calls, repairs and late rent?"
- **Answer idea:** Compare the fee with the hours, vacancy and mistakes it can save, and let them choose full management or leasing only. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Fair to compare. Want a side-by-side of managing it yourself versus with help?
- **Don't say:** 'You'll regret it.' Guarantees about vacancy or rent.
