# Starter objections: Investors

NEZ OS starters for Stage 5. Rewrite each answer in the agent's voice, swap [PROOF] for one of their proof points (or [ADD PROOF]), and add the pushback they hear most. Never promise returns, cash flow or appreciation. Tax and entity questions go to their CPA or attorney.

## 1. "The numbers don't work at that price."
- **Category:** Price
- **Behind it:** Their numbers show too little profit, or they're testing you.
- **Ask first:** "What numbers do you need to see for a deal to make sense to you?"
- **Answer idea:** Respect their numbers. Offer to run the deal with their own targets: purchase price, rehab, rent or resale, holding costs. Then show what price would make it work and whether that's realistic to offer. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Let's run it with your targets. Send me your numbers and I'll show you what price makes it work.
- **Don't say:** "It'll pay for itself." Any projected return stated as fact.

## 2. "I find my own deals."
- **Category:** Other agent
- **Behind it:** They don't see what an agent adds for a pro.
- **Ask first:** "Where do your best deals come from right now?"
- **Answer idea:** Respect their skill. Offer what saves them time: off-market or early access when you have it, fast comps, contractor and lender contacts, and handling the paperwork. [PROOF] Ask to send the next deal that fits their buy box.
- **Text:** Love that you find your own. Want me to send anything that fits your buy box before it hits the market, when I have it?
- **Don't say:** "You're missing out." Promises of off-market deals you can't deliver.

## 3. "I only want deals at 70 percent of value."
- **Category:** Price
- **Behind it:** A firm rule that protects their profit.
- **Ask first:** "Is that rule firm, or does it flex for the right property or area?"
- **Answer idea:** Agree to work inside their rule. Be honest about how often those deals show up in your market and what kind of properties they tend to be. [PROOF] Set up a search that matches it exactly.
- **Text:** I'll work inside your numbers. Want me to set up a search that only sends deals that fit?
- **Don't say:** "Nobody gets those anymore." Anything that dismisses their method.

## 4. "I want to wait for a downturn."
- **Category:** Market
- **Behind it:** They hope for lower prices.
- **Ask first:** "What would a deal need to look like for you to move now?"
- **Answer idea:** Nobody can time it. Focus on deals that work with today's numbers, and keep their criteria on file so they're ready whenever a deal fits. [PROOF]
- **Text:** No pressure. I'll keep your criteria on file and only send deals that work with today's numbers.
- **Don't say:** Market predictions.

## 5. "Why pay a commission when I can go straight to the seller?"
- **Category:** Commission & fees
- **Behind it:** They want to save money.
- **Ask first:** "What would make it worth having an agent on a deal for you?"
- **Answer idea:** Explain what you bring that affects their profit: access to deals, fast comps, negotiating, contractor and lender contacts, and handling the paperwork. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Fair question. Want me to show you what I'd bring to your next deal?
- **Don't say:** 'You'll lose money without me.' Cutting your fee on the spot.

## 6. "I'm worried about tenants and repairs."
- **Category:** Trust & process
- **Behind it:** Fear of problem tenants or a money pit.
- **Ask first:** "What happened on past deals that you don't want to repeat?"
- **Answer idea:** Build the worry into the numbers: inspection, repair budget, vacancy and management costs. Offer property manager and contractor contacts, and fair, consistent screening. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Let's build repairs and vacancy into the numbers before you buy. Want to run it together?
- **Don't say:** Promises about tenants or returns. Any screening by protected class.
