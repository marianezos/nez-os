# Starter objections: New construction

NEZ OS starters for Stage 5. Rewrite each answer in the agent's voice, swap [PROOF] for one of their proof points (or [ADD PROOF]), and add the pushback they hear most. Don't speak for the builder. Contract, warranty and lending questions go to the right professional.

## 1. "Why do I need an agent? I'll go straight to the builder."
- **Category:** Other agent
- **Behind it:** They think the builder's sales team will look out for them.
- **Ask first:** "Who do you think the builder's sales rep works for?"
- **Answer idea:** The builder's team is there for the builder. Explain what you do for the buyer: comparing builders and lots, reviewing the contract terms with them, inspections at key stages, and negotiating upgrades or closing costs where possible. [PROOF] Mention that many builders require the agent at the first visit.
- **Text:** Before your first visit to a model home, let's talk. Many builders need your agent there from day one.
- **Don't say:** That the builder will cheat them. Legal advice on the contract.

## 2. "The builder's lender gives incentives."
- **Category:** Money & readiness
- **Behind it:** They want the incentive and aren't sure it's a good deal.
- **Ask first:** "Do you know what the incentive is worth compared to another lender's offer?"
- **Answer idea:** Incentives can be worth it. Suggest comparing the builder's lender offer with one other lender side by side so they choose with real numbers. [PROOF]
- **Text:** The incentive may be great. Want to compare it with one other lender so you know for sure?
- **Don't say:** Rate quotes. "The builder's lender is always worse."

## 3. "We don't need an inspection on a new home."
- **Category:** Trust & process
- **Behind it:** They assume new means perfect.
- **Ask first:** "Would you like to know about any issues before you move in, while the builder still fixes them?"
- **Answer idea:** New homes can still have issues. Suggest inspections at key stages and before closing, so the builder handles fixes while it's easy. [PROOF]
- **Text:** New homes can still have surprises. Want me to set up an inspection before closing so the builder fixes anything first?
- **Don't say:** Claims about a specific builder's quality.

## 4. "Resale homes are cheaper."
- **Category:** Price
- **Behind it:** They're comparing price tags only.
- **Ask first:** "What matters most: price, move-in timing, or not having to fix things?"
- **Answer idea:** Compare both on the same basis: price, upgrades they'd add, repairs, timing and incentives. Help them choose what fits. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Let's compare new and resale side by side so you can see the full picture. Want me to put it together?
- **Don't say:** That one is always better.
