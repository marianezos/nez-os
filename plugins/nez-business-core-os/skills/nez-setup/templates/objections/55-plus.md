# Starter objections: 55+ communities

NEZ OS starters for Stage 5. Rewrite each answer in the agent's voice, swap [PROOF] for one of their proof points (or [ADD PROOF]), and add the pushback they hear most.

**Note:** age-restricted communities have their own Fair Housing rules. Describe the community's features, rules and amenities as the community states them. Never describe residents, and never steer anyone toward or away from a community because of age or any other protected class. Talk about the client's situation (downsizing, one-level living, less upkeep), not their age. Health, financial and legal questions go to the right professional.

## 1. "We're not ready to leave our home."
- **Category:** Timing
- **Behind it:** The home holds memories, and moving feels like a big loss.
- **Ask first:** "What would a new place need to have for it to feel like home?"
- **Answer idea:** There's no rush. Offer to show what a move could look like, like what they'd keep, what they'd gain and the timeline, so they can decide when it feels right. [PROOF]
- **Text:** No rush at all. Whenever you're curious, I can show you what a move could look like, step by step.
- **Don't say:** "At your age you should..." Any pressure or fear.

## 2. "The HOA fees are too high."
- **Category:** Money & readiness
- **Behind it:** They're comparing fees to what they pay now.
- **Ask first:** "What do you spend now on things the fee might cover, like yard work, snow and maintenance?"
- **Answer idea:** Help them compare the fee with what it covers and what they spend today. Point them to the community's documents for exact details. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Let's compare the fee with what it covers and what you spend today. Want me to pull the community's documents?
- **Don't say:** Guesses about future fee increases.

## 3. "Our kids think we should wait."
- **Category:** Trust & process
- **Behind it:** They want family on board.
- **Ask first:** "Would it help if your family joined our next conversation?"
- **Answer idea:** Welcome the family in. Offer a short call or tour where everyone can ask questions. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Family should be part of this. Want to set up a call where everyone can ask their questions?
- **Don't say:** Anything that takes sides in the family.

## 4. "We need to sell before we can buy."
- **Category:** Timing
- **Behind it:** Fear of owning two homes or having nowhere to go.
- **Ask first:** "What would make the timing feel safe for you?"
- **Answer idea:** Lay out the options for timing a sale and a purchase, and connect them with a lender about financing choices. [PROOF]
- **Text:** There are a few ways to time it so you're never stuck. Want me to walk you through them?
- **Don't say:** Lending advice or promises about timing.
