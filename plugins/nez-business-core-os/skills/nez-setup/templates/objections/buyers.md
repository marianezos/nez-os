# Starter objections: Buyers

NEZ OS starters for Stage 5. Rewrite each answer in the agent's voice, swap [PROOF] for one of their proof points (or [ADD PROOF]), and add the pushback they hear most. Never predict rates or promise a home will appraise. Lending questions go to their lender.

## 1. "We're waiting for rates to come down."
- **Category:** Market
- **Behind it:** They're worried about the monthly payment and don't want to regret the timing.
- **Ask first:** "What monthly payment would feel comfortable to you?"
- **Answer idea:** Nobody knows where rates are going. Shift to what they can control: a payment they're comfortable with today, and what changes if rates move either way. [PROOF] Offer to set them up with a lender for real numbers, and keep watching homes in their range so they're ready either way.
- **Text:** Fair to wait. Want me to connect you with a lender so you know your real payment today? Then you can decide with numbers.
- **Don't say:** "Rates are about to go up." "Buy now and refinance later" as a promise. Any rate quote.

## 2. "I don't want to sign a buyer agreement yet."
- **Category:** Trust & process
- **Behind it:** They don't want to feel locked in, or they're unsure how you get paid.
- **Ask first:** "What would you want the agreement to say for you to feel comfortable?"
- **Answer idea:** Explain that the agreement simply spells out what you'll do and how you're paid, so nothing is a surprise. Offer to read it together and adjust the terms that can be adjusted, like the length. [PROOF] Keep it calm and clear.
- **Text:** Totally fair to ask. Want to go over the agreement together for 10 minutes? You'll know exactly what you're signing.
- **Don't say:** "It's just a formality." "Everyone signs it." Anything that sounds like pressure or legal advice.

## 3. "We'll just find a house online ourselves."
- **Category:** Other agent
- **Behind it:** They think the agent's job is finding listings.
- **Ask first:** "What part of buying feels the most uncertain to you?"
- **Answer idea:** Agree the search is easy now. The hard parts are pricing an offer, winning against other offers, inspections, repairs and getting to closing. [PROOF] Offer one example of how you help after they find the home.
- **Text:** You're right, finding homes online is easy. Want to know what usually happens after you find the one? Happy to walk you through it.
- **Don't say:** "You can't do it without me." Anything that dismisses their research.

## 4. "We want to see a lot more homes first."
- **Category:** Timing
- **Behind it:** Fear of picking the wrong one.
- **Ask first:** "What would the right home need to have for you to feel sure?"
- **Answer idea:** Seeing homes is how they learn the market. Help them sort must-haves from nice-to-haves so each showing teaches them something. [PROOF] Suggest a short list for the next tour.
- **Text:** Let's make every showing count. Want to send me your top 3 must-haves before we pick the next homes?
- **Don't say:** "This one will be gone tomorrow." False urgency.

## 5. "Let's offer way under asking."
- **Category:** Price
- **Behind it:** They don't want to overpay.
- **Ask first:** "What price would make you feel like you got a fair deal?"
- **Answer idea:** Respect the goal. Show what similar homes sold for and how long this one has been listed, then decide on a strong strategy together. [PROOF] The goal is an offer that gets accepted on good terms, not the lowest number.
- **Text:** Let's look at what similar homes sold for before we set the number. Can I send you the comps?
- **Don't say:** "You'll lose it if you lowball." Guarantees about what the seller will accept.

## 6. "We'll just use the listing agent."
- **Category:** Other agent
- **Behind it:** They think it'll be simpler or get them a better deal.
- **Ask first:** "Who do you want negotiating for you on price and repairs?"
- **Answer idea:** Explain, without knocking anyone, that the listing agent works for the seller. Describe what you do for the buyer: pricing the offer, inspections, repairs and getting to closing. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Totally your choice. Want to know what having someone on your side looks like before you decide?
- **Don't say:** Anything negative about the listing agent. Claims about what they will or won't do.

## 7. "We're not sure we can afford this yet."
- **Category:** Money & readiness
- **Behind it:** They're nervous about the payment, closing costs or what comes after.
- **Ask first:** "What monthly payment would let you sleep at night?"
- **Answer idea:** Connect them with a lender for real numbers, and lay out the full cost of buying (down payment, closing costs, moving, repairs) so nothing surprises them. [PROOF]
- **Text:** Let's get you real numbers, not guesses. Want an intro to a lender this week?
- **Don't say:** 'You can definitely afford it.' Any rate or approval promise.
