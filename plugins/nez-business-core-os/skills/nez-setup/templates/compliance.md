# Compliance Guardrails: how to fill the page (Stage 2)

The **Compliance Guardrails** page already exists in the brain (`compliance_page_id`). Its rule sections (Fair Housing, Advertising, Texting email and data, Professional limits) are already written. Setup fills only the **My details** lines and adds to **My state and brokerage rules**. Never rewrite the rule sections. If the page is missing, follow the brain rules' safety net and recreate it with the sections listed below.

## My details lines

Under **My state and brokerage rules**, add one bullet per rule from question 10 (and any rule from question 8 that's a "never do" instead of a detail). Keep the placeholder bullet if they have none.

## Rule sections on the page (for recreating it)

Fair Housing; Advertising; Texting, email and data; Professional limits; My state and brokerage rules. The current wording lives in the NEZ OS Brain template. If you have to recreate the page, write those headings with these points in your own short wording:

- **Fair Housing:** never describe, target, include or exclude anyone by a protected class (federal, plus the listed states and cities); describe homes by features and what's nearby; no steering words; answer "who is it good for" with features; no "good," "bad" or "safe" about schools or areas.
- **Advertising:** short disclosure line on captions, ads, social posts and video descriptions, full line on emails, newsletters, web pages, blogs and landing pages; include the listed notices where the agent uses them and never claim what the law requires; housing ads on Facebook and Instagram use the Housing special ad category, so no age, gender or zip targeting; no promises, guaranteed prices or sale dates, no quoting rates; every stat has a source and date, estimates are labeled; no school ratings, crime data or flood zones as fact; only reviews marked Approved to Use; private proof stays out of public pieces.
- **Texting, email and data:** text only with consent, and if consent is "no" or "not sure" don't suggest text campaigns; opt-out and unsubscribe language; only data the agent gives or is licensed to use, follow MLS rules, no scraping; drafts only, never send or post without the agent's OK.
- **Professional limits:** tax, legal and lending are preparation for their CPA, attorney or lender, never advice.

## Five-point summary to show the agent

1. Homes are described by features and what's nearby, never by who lives there or who they "suit."
2. Housing ads on Facebook and Instagram use the Housing special ad category: no age, gender or zip targeting.
3. Every public piece ends with your disclosure line (short for captions and ads, full for emails and web pages), plus any notices you listed.
4. No promises, no quoted rates, no school, crime or flood claims as fact, and private proof stays private.
5. I draft, you approve. I check texting consent before any outreach, and tax, legal and lending questions go to your pros.
