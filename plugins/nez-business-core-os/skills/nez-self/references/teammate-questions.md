# Copy-and-paste questions for a teammate

Used by `teammates.md`. Give the agent everything inside the code block, with [AGENT] (the agent's name) and [NAME] (the teammate's name) filled in. Don't add to it or change the questions. It takes about 10 minutes, uses work language, and has no wrong answers. When the answers come back, score them with the guide at the bottom.

```
Hi [NAME]! I'm setting up my AI system so the way I work with you fits how you like to work: messages, plans and handoffs. These questions are only about how you work. There are no wrong answers, and you can skip anything you'd rather not answer.

Pick the letter that sounds most like you. If two fit, write both and say which is stronger. Add a note to any answer if you like. It takes about 10 minutes.

HOW YOU WORK

1. You have a big work decision to make. You usually:
A) Decide fast and adjust later
B) Talk it through with someone
C) Make sure everyone's on board first
D) Research it before deciding

2. Something goes wrong at work. Your first move:
A) Fix it and move on
B) Tell someone so I can think out loud
C) Check that the people involved are okay
D) Find out exactly what happened first

3. In a meeting, you're usually the one who:
A) Pushes for a decision
B) Keeps the energy up
C) Makes sure everyone's heard
D) Asks the careful questions

4. Feedback works best for you when it's:
A) Direct and blunt
B) Friendly and conversational
C) Gentle and thoughtful
D) Detailed, with examples

5. You do your best work when:
A) It's fast and intense
B) I'm around people
C) The day is steady and predictable
D) I get long stretches of focus

6. Starting something new, you:
A) Jump right in
B) Get excited and tell people
C) Want support and a plan first
D) Map it all out first

7. When you're overloaded, you:
A) Push through
B) Talk to someone
C) Slow down and take care of myself and others
D) Step back and make a plan

8. What kills your motivation fastest:
A) Slow progress or being micromanaged
B) Working alone with no feedback
C) Conflict or feeling unsupported
D) Rushing without a clear plan

HOW YOU LIKE TO GET INFORMATION

9. A message you'll actually read is:
A) Two or three lines, bottom line first
B) Short and warm, main point up front
C) Clear and friendly, and tells me what happens next
D) Complete, with the details and the reasons

10. You'd rather get things as:
A) Bullet points
B) A quick story or example
C) Steps in order
D) A table or the numbers

11. When I ask you to do something, lead with:
A) What I need and by when
B) Why it matters and who it helps
C) What help you'll have
D) The background and how I got there

12. How direct should I be?
A) Very direct
B) Direct but friendly
C) Gentle, with room to talk
D) Direct and backed by facts

13. How much detail do you want, usually?
A) Just the headline
B) Enough to get the idea
C) Enough to feel comfortable
D) Everything, so I can check it

14. For an update you don't need to act on, you'd like:
A) Nothing unless something changes
B) A quick note
C) A short check-in
D) A written summary

15. Best way for me to reach you:
A) Text
B) Call
C) Email
D) Team chat app

OPTIONAL: YOUR ENERGY AND DECISIONS (skip if you like)

16. When opportunities come up, you usually:
A) Go after them myself
B) Respond to what shows up
C) Wait to be asked
D) Try different things before committing

17. Your energy during the day is:
A) Steady all day
B) In waves
C) Shaped by the people around me
D) Quiet and focused

18. Your best decisions come from:
A) A gut feeling right away
B) Sleeping on it
C) Talking it through
D) Waiting until it feels right

19. After a full day with people, you feel:
A) Energized
B) Good, but ready to rest
C) Drained
D) It depends on the people

OPTIONAL: TEST RESULTS (only if you want to share)

20. If you've taken a personality or strengths test (DISC, Enneagram, 16 Personalities, CliftonStrengths, Kolbe), you can share the result here.
For Human Design, share only your type and authority (a free chart tool shows both). Please don't send your birth date, time or place.

21. Anything else that helps you do your best work?

Thank you! Please send your answers back to [AGENT].
```

## Scoring guide (for the AI, never sent)

- **Questions 1 to 8:** count the letters. A = Driver, B = Connector, C = Steady, D = Planner. The highest count is their main style, the next is their second. Within one point = a blend (for example "Steady-Planner"). When they wrote two letters, the stronger one counts fully; the other only breaks ties.
- **Questions 9 to 15:** don't score. Read each one straight into Communication Tips, one short line each: **Length** (9), **Format** (10), **Lead with** (11), **Directness** (12), **Detail** (13), **Updates** (14). Question 15 fills **Best Way to Reach** in People (A Text, B Call, C Email; D: write "Team chat" in Notes).
- **Questions 16 to 19:** tendencies only (A starts things, B responds, C guides when asked, D explores first). Write them in their page's "What helps them do their best work" as approximate, and never present them as a Human Design chart.
- **Question 20:** save each result in one line under "Other results," or "Not shared." Never save birth details, even if they send them.
- **Question 21:** add to "What helps them do their best work" in their words.
- Skipped questions stay blank. Never fill a gap with a guess.
