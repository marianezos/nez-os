# Person page layout (People database)

The page inside each People row. Write it in this order. Fill in only what you know; write "Not shared" for anything the person didn't answer or share. Keep it short: it's a guide for working together, not a file on the person.

**First line:** "Profile for [name], [profile date]. Source: [their own answers | Estimated by [agent's name]]. Run /nez-self to update it."

## Role and reporting
Their role, who they report to, Human or AI, and status (Active, Waiting for answers, Former).

## Work style
Main and second style (or the blend) in one line, then 2 to 3 sentences on what it means day to day, from their answers.

## How they communicate
How they explain things, how they like feedback, and how they show up in a group or meeting (questions 3, 4 and 9 to 12).

## How they work and decide
How they make decisions, what their energy looks like during the day, and how they start new things, in plain words (questions 1, 5, 6 and 16 to 19).

## How to communicate with them
The six Communication Tips lines, the same as the field:
- **Length:**
- **Format:**
- **Lead with:**
- **Directness:**
- **Detail:**
- **Updates:**

## What helps them do their best work
2 to 4 bullets from their answers: what drives them, their ideal setup, anything they added in question 21.

## Watch for
1 to 2 kind bullets on what drains them or slows them down (questions 7 and 8), and how to help.

## Energy pattern and other results
The same as the Other Results field: any test they chose to share (for example "Enneagram 2"). For Human Design, the type and authority, labeled "their own chart" or "approximate." Otherwise "Not shared." Never a birth date, time or place.

## Notes
Anything the person asked the team to know about working with them, in their words. Nothing about health, family, religion or age.

## Their answers
The answers exactly as they gave them (question number and letter, plus any notes), or the agent's answers if the profile is estimated, so the profile can be checked or redone later.
