# Person page layout (People database)

The page inside each People row. Write it in this order. Keep it short: it's a guide for working together, not a file on the person.

First line: "Estimated by [agent's name]" if Profile Source is Estimated. Otherwise "Their own answers, [date]."

## Work style
Main and second style in one line, then 2 to 3 sentences on what it means day to day.

## How to communicate with them
The five Communication Tips lines: length, format, detail, directness, what to lead with.

## What helps them do their best work
2 to 4 bullets from their answers (what drives them, their ideal day, anything they added).

## Watch for
1 to 2 kind bullets on what drains them or slows them down, and how to help.

## Other results
Any test they chose to share (for example "Enneagram 2" or "Human Design: Projector, Splenic"), or "Not shared."

For the agent's own row ("Me"), copy the summary from the Master Profile's My personality section and add: "Full profile: Master Profile, My personality."
