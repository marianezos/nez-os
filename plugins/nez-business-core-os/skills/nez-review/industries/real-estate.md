# Review: real estate

Used by `SKILL.md`. Follow its core rules: use only the pay structure saved by /nez-bizplan, show every step, and never assume. Real estate agents work with many kinds of clients, so review every number by the client types they actually serve.

## What to read from My business numbers

- **The goal:** Goal type, Goal number, Plan period, Closings needed, Milestone or award.
- **So far this year:** This year so far: closings, This year so far: GCI.
- **The funnel and rates:** Leads last year, Agreements signed last year, Closed from those agreements, Average sale price, Time from first contact to closing.
- **The mix:** My business mix, plus Client types I work with (My clients).
- **The pay structure:** Split and cap, Cap year starts and cap paid so far, Fees on each deal, After the cap, Franchise or royalty fee, Monthly or yearly brokerage fees, Team split, Referral fees, Costs outside my brokerage, My commission per side, Team deals count toward my goal, Deal walk-through (confirmed), Take-home per deal.
- **Time:** Hours per week for finding business.
- **Earlier reviews:** Last review date, Review period, Lead source spend.

## The numbers to compare

Use the names the plan uses: **conversations, appointments, agreements signed, closings, GCI**, and take-home if the pay structure is saved. Name appointments the way each client type works: buyer consultations, listing appointments, lease showings, investor meetings.

## By client type

For each type in Client types I work with that the plan includes: closings and GCI planned (from My business mix and the plan) against actual (check-in lines that name the type, and the Type on Closed deals). Name one type ahead and one behind with the numbers. Group the rest as "other client types." A type with no data is a gap.

## Take-home (for the actual closings)

1. **GCI per closing:** from Clients & Deals (Price × My commission per side), the check-ins, or the agent. If only a total is known, divide by closings and label it an average.
2. **Only what's saved.** Never use a fee the agent didn't give, and never fill one in from what you know about a brokerage. Missing line: ask once with the matching /nez-bizplan question (P1 to P11), save it, and go on. "None" is zero; "not sure" isn't counted, and you say so.
3. **Walk the closings in date order.** Start from how much cap was left at the start of the timeframe (Cap year starts, cap paid so far, plus closings since). Say which closing reached the cap and which reached any royalty cap, and show each closing's take-home before or after, in the order on Deal walk-through (confirmed). Subtract monthly or yearly fees for the months in the timeframe, and the outside costs they wanted counted.
4. **Side by side:** GCI and take-home, then the same planned for the timeframe. If the cap year resets inside the timeframe, show before and after the reset exactly as they described it. Anything unclear: ask.
5. **Nothing saved:** show GCI only and say: "Take-home needs your split and fees. Run /nez-bizplan to add them and I'll show both."

**Team leaders:** if Team deals count toward my goal is yes, review team totals, and offer the same view per agent.

## Lead sources

Use Where my business comes from (My clients) and Top lead sources on Business Plan & Goals as the list, plus the Lead Source on Clients & Deals rows. Use only sources the agent has. Sphere, referrals and past clients usually have no cost, which is fine: show "no cost entered." Ask once for monthly spend for paid sources (ads, portal leads, open house costs).

## Rules for real estate reviews

- Numbers and activities only. Never describe people, neighborhoods or schools, and never rate an area.
- Never promise or predict a closing, a price or a timeline. Say what the numbers show.
- Never quote a rate, market statistic or industry average as fact. Their own numbers only.
- Tax and legal questions go to their CPA or attorney. A review isn't tax advice.
- Commission figures stay on the Business Reviews page, never in anything public.
