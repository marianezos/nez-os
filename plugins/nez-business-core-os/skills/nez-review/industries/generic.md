# Review: any business

Used by `SKILL.md` when `industry_flow` isn't `real-estate`. Follow its core rules: use only the pricing and costs saved by /nez-bizplan, show every step, and use their own word for what they sell ("clients", "loans", "policies", "projects"). Below, "clients" stands for their word.

## What to read from My business numbers

- **The goal:** Goal type, Goal number, Plan period, Clients needed, Milestone or award.
- **So far this year:** This year so far: clients, This year so far: revenue.
- **The funnel and rates:** Leads last year, Calls or proposals last year, Became clients, Average value per client, Time from first contact to closing.
- **The mix:** My business mix (by offer or service, if they split it).
- **The costs:** Fees on each sale, Payment processing, Partner and referral fees, Monthly or yearly costs, Other costs, What I keep after costs, Deal walk-through (confirmed), Take-home per client.
- **Capacity and time:** My capacity, Hours per week for finding business.
- **Earlier reviews:** Last review date, Review period, Lead source spend.

## The numbers to compare

Use the names the plan uses: **leads or conversations, calls or proposals, clients won, revenue**, and take-home if costs are saved.

## By offer

If they split their business by offer or service, review each one: clients and revenue planned against actual, and name the one ahead and the one behind. If they didn't, say so, and offer to review by offer if they give rough shares now. An offer with no data is a gap.

## Take-home (for the actual sales)

1. **Revenue per sale:** from the check-ins or the agent. If only a total is known, divide by sales and label it an average.
2. **Only what's saved,** in the order on Deal walk-through (confirmed). Never use a fee they didn't give, and never fill one in from what you know about a platform or processor. Missing line: ask once with the matching /nez-bizplan generic question (P1 to P5), save it, and go on. "None" is zero; "not sure" isn't counted.
3. Show one sale at the average value as a short table, then multiply by the sales in the timeframe, subtract monthly or yearly costs for the months in the timeframe and other costs they wanted counted, and show revenue and take-home side by side next to the plan.
4. **Only a percent saved** (What I keep after costs): use it and label it (your estimate).
5. **Nothing saved:** show revenue only and say: "Take-home needs your fees and costs. Run /nez-bizplan to add them and I'll show both."

## Capacity

Compare clients per month with My capacity. Over capacity: say wins are being held back by time, not leads, and offer options (raise prices, add help, narrow the offer). Under capacity and behind: the bottleneck is activity or conversion.

## Lead sources

Use Where my business comes from and Top lead sources as the list. Only sources they have. Ask once for monthly spend on paid sources. A source with no cost shows "no cost entered."

## Rules for any-business reviews

- Numbers and activities only. No promises about results, income or timelines.
- Never quote a benchmark or industry average as fact.
- Income figures stay on the Business Reviews page, never in anything public.
- Tax, legal and financial questions go to their accountant, attorney or advisor.
