# Review page layout

Reviews live on the agent's **Business Reviews** page (`business_reviews_page_id`, inside the Business Plan & Goals page). Never create a database. The page keeps its callout at the top, then one section per review, newest first.

## One section per review

A heading 2 with the period and the review date, for example **2026 Q4 so far (reviewed Nov 17, 2026)**. A rerun of the same period replaces that section; never add a second one, and never touch another review. Under the heading, these parts in this order. Skip a part only when the data can't support it, and write one line saying why.

1. **Timeframe:** what was reviewed (this quarter so far, last quarter, year to date), the dates, the review date, how many check-ins and closed deals it used, and the status line it used (10% unless the agent picked another).
2. **Since last review:** skip on the first review. Each adjustment from the last review with Done, Partly or Not yet, the number it watched (then and now), and what got in the way if it didn't happen.
3. **Plan vs actual:** a table, one row per number: Plan to date, Actual, Difference, Status. Then one line for anything not counted yet (under contract deals and their expected months).
4. **Take-home:** GCI or revenue next to take-home for the actual closings, before and after the cap, next to the plan. List any fee not counted and why.
5. **By client type:** a table of Plan, Actual and Status for each type. List any gap.
6. **By lead source:** a table of Leads, Agreements, Closings, Lead to close rate and Cost per closing (if known). Label comparisons (from your own numbers) and estimated counts (your estimate).
7. **What's working, what isn't:** up to three of each, each with its numbers. Then **The bottleneck:** one line.
8. **Keep, fix, drop:** three short lists, with where freed time or money goes.
9. **Adjustments:** up to three, each with what to change, why, and what to watch next week.
10. **Gaps:** what the review couldn't cover and what would unlock it.

## The link on Business Plan & Goals

In the This quarter section, keep exactly one line:
`Latest review: <link to the review section> (reviewed <date>)`
Replace it on each new review. If the reviewed quarter has a section on the Quarterly Goals page, keep the same single line in its Review part. Touch nothing else on either page.

## Rules

- Plain words, a number next to every claim.
- Never a promise about results.
- Commission and income figures stay on this page; never put them in anything public.
- No em dashes.
