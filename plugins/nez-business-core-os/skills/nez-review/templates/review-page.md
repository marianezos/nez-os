# Review page layout

Reviews live on the agent's **Business Reviews** page (`business_reviews_page_id`, inside the Business Plan & Goals page). Never create a database. The page keeps its callout at the top, then one section per review, newest first.

## One section per review

A heading 2 with the period and the review date, for example **2026 Q4 so far (reviewed Nov 17, 2026)**. A rerun of the same period replaces that section; never add a second one, and never touch another review. Under the heading, these parts in this order. Skip a part only when the data can't support it, and write one line saying why.

1. **Timeframe:** what was reviewed (this quarter so far, last quarter, year to date), the dates, the review date, how many check-ins and closed deals it used, and the status line it used (10% unless the agent picked another).
2. **Plan vs actual:** a table, one row per number: Plan to date, Actual, Difference, Status. Then one line for anything not counted yet (under contract deals and their expected months).
3. **Take-home:** GCI or revenue next to take-home for the actual closings, before and after the cap, next to the plan. List any fee not counted and why.
4. **By client type:** a table of Plan, Actual and Status for each type. List any gap.
5. **By lead source:** a table of Leads, Agreements, Closings, Lead to close rate and Cost per closing (if known). Label comparisons (from your own numbers) and estimated counts (your estimate).
6. **What's working, what isn't:** up to three of each, each with its numbers. Then **The bottleneck:** one line.
7. **Keep, fix, drop:** three short lists, with where freed time or money goes.
8. **Adjustments:** up to three, each with what to change, why, and what to watch next week.
9. **Gaps:** what the review couldn't cover and what would unlock it.

## The link on Business Plan & Goals

In the This quarter section, keep exactly one line:
`Latest review: <link to the review section> (reviewed <date>)`
Replace it on each new review. Touch nothing else on that page.

## Rules

- Plain words, a number next to every claim.
- Never a promise about results.
- Commission and income figures stay on this page; never put them in anything public.
- No em dashes.
